Follow the steps

Unbilled work or outstanding on-call days under a reviewed agreement.

  1. Open Practice, then Work and select the agreement. Choose the Invoice button for unbilled entries or outstanding on-call days.
  2. In Which days go on this invoice?, select the days you intend to bill and check the displayed total.
  3. Open Invoice preview. Review the dates, descriptions, rates and TOTAL DUE against your records.
  4. Choose Send invoice… and the available format, or Copy to prepare the text for your own message. Complete the chosen sharing step and verify the recipient.
  5. Open Practice, then Invoices and check the invoice record. Follow up on delivery through the sharing method you used.

Good to know

  • Both sending and Copy mark the selected entries as billed in the app. Copy alone does not deliver an invoice to the agency.
  • If the preview is wrong, close it and correct the work or agreement before sharing.
  • An invoice marked billed or sent does not mean it has been paid.

You’re done when: A reviewed invoice is recorded, and you know how it was shared.
