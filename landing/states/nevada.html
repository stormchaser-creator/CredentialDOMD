i Deadlines, fees, and what happens if you are late

When it is due
MD: biennial registration due on or before June 30 of each odd-numbered year (or next business day if June 30 falls on a weekend or holiday), per NRS 630.267; the current registration period runs July 1, 2025 through June 30, 2027, so the next MD renewal is due June 30, 2027. DO: renewal due on or before December 31 (NRS 633.471); per the board's Summer 2025 newsletter, the last annual deadline was December 31, 2025, and starting in 2026 DO licenses renew in every even-numbered year for a two-year period, making December 31, 2026 the next DO renewal deadline.
Fee
MD: $750 biennial registration for an active MD ($375 inactive; $375 if renewing for only the second half of the biennium), per the board's Licensure Fees schedule; statutory cap is $1,000 (NRS 630.268). DO: $350 renewal fee ($200 inactive) on the fee schedule effective July 1, 2023; the board's Summer 2025 newsletter states DO renewal fees will increase effective with the first biennial renewal, with the revised chart to be posted (statutory cap for the biennial DO renewal fee is $1,000, NRS 633.501). A 2.5% card-processing fee applies to MD-board credit card payments.
Late fee
MD: no separate late fee; a lapsed license must be reinstated at twice the current biennial registration fee, which the fee schedule lists as $1,500 for active MD reinstatement (NRS 630.267(2)). DO: late fee of $200 for active DOs and $150 for inactive DOs per the board fee schedule (statutory cap $300, NRS 633.501(1)(h)).
If you miss the deadline
MD: no grace period. If the biennial fee and required registration information are not submitted when due, the license expires (NRS 630.267(2)). Within 2 years after expiration the physician may be reinstated by paying twice the current biennial registration fee ($1,500 active per the fee schedule), submitting all registration information, and being found in good standing; after 2 years the license cannot be renewed or reinstated and a new license application is required. The board reports licenses that expired during the year to the DEA and the State Board of Pharmacy by September 30 of the odd-numbered year, and reinstatement must be to the status held before expiration. DO: if renewal requirements are not met within 10 days after the renewal date, the board sends a 15-day notice by certified mail; if the license is not renewed within 15 days after receipt of notice, it expires automatically without further notice or hearing, and the expiration is filed with the DEA (NRS 633.481). Restoration requires payment of all past-due renewal fees plus the late payment fee (NRS 633.501), verified CME for the period preceding the renewal date and each period since expiration, and a sworn statement that no disqualifying information was withheld.
MD vs DO
This state runs separate MD and DO requirements. MD: 40 hours per 2-year cycle. at least 40 Category 1, 2 hrs pain management, 2 hrs controlled substances, 2 hrs suicide prevention, 2 hrs cultural competency. DO: 35 hours per year. at least 10 Category 1, 2 hrs controlled substances, 2 hrs suicide prevention, 2 hrs pain management, 2 hrs cultural competency.

1 Step-by-Step: Renewing Your Nevada Medical License

Follow these steps to renew your medical license with the Nevada State Board of Medical Examiners:

  1. Complete your CME for the cycle (40 hours) (see details below)
  2. Log in to the Nevada State Board of Medical Examiners renewal portal
  3. Complete the renewal application and answer the attestation questions
  4. Pay the renewal fee (MD: $750 biennial registration for an active MD ($375 inactive; $375 if renewing for only the second half of the biennium), per the board's Licensure Fees schedule; statutory cap is $1,000 (NRS 630)
  5. Submit and save your confirmation

2 Nevada CME Requirements

MD: 40 hours per 2-year cycle. at least 40 Category 1, 2 hrs pain management, 2 hrs controlled substances, 2 hrs suicide prevention, 2 hrs cultural competency. DO: 35 hours per year. at least 10 Category 1, 2 hrs controlled substances, 2 hrs suicide prevention, 2 hrs pain management, 2 hrs cultural competency.

3 Common Pitfalls to Avoid

These are the mistakes Nevada physicians most commonly make during renewal:

  • Missing the MD June 30 odd-year deadline doubles the cost immediately: the license expires with no grace period and reinstatement is twice the biennial fee, $1,500 instead of $750 (NRS 630.267(2); board Licensure Fees PDF). After 2 years expired, reinstatement is impossible and a full new application is required.
  • Expired Nevada MD licenses are reported to the DEA and the Nevada State Board of Pharmacy by September 30 of the renewal year (NRS 630.267(5)), so a lapse quickly ripples into federal and state prescribing registrations.
  • MDs and DOs are on completely different clocks: MDs renew by June 30 of odd-numbered years, DOs by December 31 and now only in even-numbered years under AB 56 (2025). Physicians moving between the boards or holding dual credentials must track both anchors.
  • DO renewal notices are emailed from noreply@thentiacloud.com, not a bom.nv.gov address. The board's Summer 2025 newsletter warns these can land in spam and that failure to keep your email current may result in late payment fees; late fees were assessed for anyone who missed December 31, 2025.
  • The MD board cannot accept personal or business checks: payment must be cashier's check, money order, or credit card, and credit cards carry a nonrefundable 2.5% processing fee (Licensure Fees PDF).
  • The DO lapse window is short: 10 days after the renewal date triggers a 15-day certified-mail notice, and the license then expires automatically without a hearing (NRS 633.481). Restoration requires all past-due fees, the late fee, and verified CME for every period since expiration.

4 Frequently Asked Questions

When is my Nevada MD license renewal due? ▾
On or before June 30 of every odd-numbered year (or the next business day if June 30 falls on a weekend or holiday), per NRS 630.267. The current registration period is July 1, 2025 through June 30, 2027, so the next renewal deadline is June 30, 2027.
How much does MD renewal cost? ▾
$750 for the full biennium for an active MD; $375 for an inactive MD. If you are registering for only the second half of the biennium, the fee is $375 (active). Credit card payments add a 2.5% processing fee, and personal or business checks are not accepted. Source: the board's Licensure Fees PDF.
What happens if I miss the MD renewal deadline? ▾
Your license expires immediately; there is no grace period. Within 2 years you can be reinstated by paying twice the current biennial fee ($1,500 active per the fee schedule) and submitting all registration information, provided you are in good standing. After 2 years you must apply for a brand-new license. The board also reports expired licenses to the DEA and State Board of Pharmacy by September 30 of the renewal year (NRS 630.267).
When do Nevada DO licenses renew, and what changed? ▾
DO renewal is due on or before December 31. Under AB 56 (signed May 26, 2025), the last annual renewal deadline was December 31, 2025; starting in 2026, DO licenses renew every even-numbered year for a two-year period, so the next deadline is December 31, 2026. DO CME also changes to 40 hours every two years beginning January 1, 2027. Source: NRS 633.471 and the board's Summer 2025 newsletter.
Where do I renew online? ▾
MDs: the Board of Medical Examiners licensee portal at https://nsbme.us.thentiacloud.net/webs/nsbme/service/#/login. DOs: the Osteopathic Board's Thentia licensee portal at https://nsbom.portalus.thentiacloud.net/webs/portal/service/#/login, where you renew, pay fees, and upload CME certificates.
What must I submit with renewal besides the fee? ▾
MDs must submit a list of all malpractice or negligence actions filed or claims submitted to arbitration or mediation against them during the previous 2 years, plus all information required to complete the biennial registration (NRS 630.267). DOs likewise submit a malpractice/claims list for the previous period, complete renewal forms, and attest to CME; the DO board requires CME certificates to be uploaded to the portal before the end of each renewal year, and audited licensees who cannot document CME face automatic expiration under NRS 633.481.
