i Deadlines, fees, and what happens if you are late

When it is due
Fixed date: all physician licenses must be renewed no later than June 30 of each calendar year, and each renewal carries the license through the next June 30 (Board rule, Ch. 1 Sec. 10; W.S. 33-26-305 requires annual lapse on a board-set date). First-time renewal depends on issue date: licenses issued July 1 through February 28/29 renew by the immediately following June 30; licenses issued March 1 through June 30 renew by June 30 of the next calendar year.
Fee
$155 (annual physician renewal, incl. administrative license, per Board fee schedule PDF effective January 30, 2023). EXCEPTION: the Board entirely waived the renewal fee for the 2026-2027 renewal year during its software conversion; paper renewals still pay a $25 paper processing fee. IMLC-issued licenses renewed via IMLCC paid $0 to Wyoming this year plus the IMLCC $25 processing fee.
Late fee
$100 license renewal grace period surcharge (fee schedule PDF). During the current 2026 grace period the Board quotes $125 total for paper renewal: $100 surcharge plus $25 paper processing fee.
If you miss the deadline
90-day grace period: a physician who misses June 30 may still renew by submitting the renewal application, renewal fee, and $100 grace period surcharge no later than September 30 (Board rule Ch. 1 Sec. 10). For 2026 the Board states all grace period renewals and fees must be RECEIVED in the Board office by 5:00 p.m. on September 30, 2026, and grace period renewal is by paper form. After the grace period the license lapses. Reactivation of a lapsed license (rule Ch. 1 Sec. 12) requires a board application, applicable fees ($250 reactivation per fee schedule, which covers an NPDB report, criminal record check if necessary, and license through the next June 30), two references, and proof of at least 60 hours of qualified CME within the preceding 3 years; the Board may require a licensure interview, and without one reactivation requires a majority vote of the Board. Reinstatement is a separate $300 pathway on the fee schedule, and costs may also be imposed under Ch. 4 Sec. 3(k).
Processing time
Up to 7 business days after completing the 2026-2027 online renewal form for it to be fully processed and a license mailed (Board's own renewal page, June 2026). No published processing time for paper or grace period renewals.

1 Step-by-Step: Renewing Your Wyoming Medical License

Follow these steps to renew your medical license with the Wyoming Board of Medicine:

  1. Complete your CME for the cycle (60 hours) (see details below)
  2. Log in to the Wyoming Board of Medicine renewal portal
  3. Complete the renewal application and answer the attestation questions
  4. Pay the renewal fee ($155 (annual physician renewal, incl)
  5. Submit and save your confirmation

2 Wyoming CME Requirements

60 hours per 3-year cycle.

3 Common Pitfalls to Avoid

These are the mistakes Wyoming physicians most commonly make during renewal:

  • Wyoming renews EVERY year on June 30, not biennially. The CME reporting cycle is 3 years, which misleads physicians into assuming a multi-year license; the license itself lapses annually (W.S. 33-26-305; Board rule Ch. 1 Sec. 10).
  • The 2026-2027 renewal window is already CLOSED (it ended 5:00 p.m. June 30, 2026). Until September 30, 2026 the only path is the paper grace period renewal mailed to the Board office with $125 ($100 surcharge + $25 paper processing fee), and it must be RECEIVED, not postmarked, by 5:00 p.m. September 30, 2026.
  • The Board is mid software conversion: the 2026-2027 renewal ran on a Google Docs form linked from the Board's renewal page, and the old GLSuite portal page (wybomv7prod.glsuite.us) now says all 2026 physician renewals are done by paper. Do not hunt for the legacy online login.
  • Physicians licensed via the IMLC (Wyoming license number ends in the letter C) cannot renew on the Board's form alone: they must renew through https://imlcc.com/renew/ first, after which Board staff email a link to a required Board renewal form. Both steps are required.
  • The renewal fee waiver is a one-time 2026-2027 event tied to the software conversion. The standing fee is $155 per year, all fees are non-refundable, and paper filings always add $25.
  • If the license lapses after September 30, reactivation is not a formality: $250 fee, two references, proof of 60 hours of CME within the preceding 3 years, a new NPDB report, and possibly a licensure interview or a majority vote of the Board.

4 Frequently Asked Questions

When does my Wyoming physician license expire? ▾
June 30 of every calendar year. All physician licenses (other than temporary and training licenses) lapse annually, and every renewal carries the license through the next June 30. First-time renewal timing depends on when the license was issued: issued July 1 to February 28/29, renew by the immediately following June 30; issued March 1 to June 30, renew by June 30 of the next calendar year.
How much does renewal cost? ▾
The standing fee is $155 per year (Board fee schedule effective 1-30-2023), plus $25 if you renew on a paper form. For the 2026-2027 renewal year only, the Board entirely waived the renewal fee while it converts software systems; paper renewals still owed the $25 processing fee.
I missed the June 30 deadline. Can I still renew? ▾
Yes, during a 90-day grace period ending September 30, with a $100 grace period surcharge added to the renewal fee. For 2026, grace period renewal is by paper form only ($125 total: $100 surcharge plus $25 paper fee) and must be received in the Board office no later than 5:00 p.m. on September 30, 2026.
What happens if I miss the September 30 grace deadline? ▾
The license lapses and you must apply for reactivation: a Board application, the $250 reactivation fee (covers an NPDB report and criminal record check if necessary), two references, and proof of at least 60 hours of qualified CME completed within the preceding 3 years. The Board may require a licensure interview; without one, reactivation requires a majority vote of the Board.
My Wyoming license came through the Interstate Medical Licensure Compact. Do I renew differently? ▾
Yes. If your license number ends in the letter C, you must renew directly through the IMLCC website (https://imlcc.com/renew/). Wyoming's fee this year is $0 and the IMLCC charges a $25 processing fee. After the IMLCC notifies the Board, Board staff email you a link to complete a required Board renewal form. Both steps are required.
How do I renew online, and how long does processing take? ▾
Start at the Board's Renew License page (https://wyomedboard.wyo.gov/physicians/renew-license). Because the Board is converting software systems, the 2026-2027 renewal used a Google Docs form linked from that page rather than the old online portal. The Board states it may take up to 7 business days after completing the form for the renewal to be fully processed and a license mailed.
