i Deadlines, fees, and what happens if you are late

When it is due
License anniversary: under RSA 310:8, II, "Licenses issued by the office shall be valid for 2 years from the date of issuance." The completed renewal application, fee, and supporting documents must be filed on or before the expiration of the license (RSA 310:8, III). OPLC must send renewal notice at least 2 months before expiration (RSA 310:8, IV), and the OPLC renewal page says licensees get an email renewal notification 90 days before expiration.
Fee
$413.00 (Physician/Administrative Physician renewal, per the OPLC Board of Medicine fee page; includes the mandatory $28.00 Professional Health Program fee). Compact Physician renewal is also $413.00.
If you miss the deadline
No grace period and no late-renewal window. Under RSA 310:8, III, if the completed renewal, fee, and documents are not filed by the expiration date the license expires and "the holders of an expired license are not authorized to practice until the licenses have been reinstated." Reinstatement fee for a physician is $413.00 ($378.00 for a Compact Physician) per the OPLC fee page. Expired licenses lapse one year from expiration; after that the holder cannot renew and must reinstate by submitting an application and meeting eligibility requirements in statute and board rules.

1 Step-by-Step: Renewing Your New Hampshire Medical License

Follow these steps to renew your medical license with the New Hampshire Board of Medicine (under the Office of Professional Licensure and Certification, OPLC):

  1. Complete your CME for the cycle (100 hours) (see details below)
  2. Log in to the New Hampshire Board of Medicine (under the Office of Professional Licensure and Certification, OPLC) renewal portal
  3. Complete the renewal application and answer the attestation questions
  4. Pay the renewal fee ($413)
  5. Submit and save your confirmation

3 Common Pitfalls to Avoid

These are the mistakes New Hampshire physicians most commonly make during renewal:

  • There is no late-renewal option or grace period in New Hampshire. Miss the expiration date and the license expires immediately, practice authorization stops, and you must reinstate (physician reinstatement fee $413). After one year an expired license lapses entirely (RSA 310:8, III).
  • Paper renewals are not used; renewal is through the New Hampshire Online Licensing portal, and first-time online renewers must create a Customer Portal account using a registration code that arrives on the renewal notification. If you never received the code you must email CustomerSupport@oplc.nh.gov with your name, phone, and license number to get one.
  • Physicians renewing an active-status license must complete the NH Physician Licensure Survey (or the opt-out form) as a condition of renewal under RSA 126-A:5, XVIII-a; it is hosted by DHHS, not on the OPLC renewal application. Inactive-status and administrative licenses are exempt.
  • All Board of Medicine continuing education is now tracked through CE Broker (mandatory for medicine as of February 3, 2025). When creating a CE Broker account, OPLC instructs licensees to search by license type, not by board.
  • Compact (IMLC-issued) physician licenses have their own renewal instructions document from the board and a different reinstatement fee ($378 vs $413), so compact licensees should not assume the standard physician renewal path applies unchanged.
  • The $413 renewal fee includes a mandatory $28 Professional Health Program (PHP) fee; older OPLC pages still show stale amounts (the licensure-requirements page lists a $300 application fee), so always use the current fee page.

4 Frequently Asked Questions

How often do I renew my New Hampshire physician license, and when is it due? ▾
Every 2 years. Under RSA 310:8 your license is valid for 2 years from its date of issuance, and the completed renewal application, fee, and any supporting documents must be filed on or before the license expiration date. OPLC sends an email renewal notification 90 days before expiration and is required by statute to notify you at least 2 months before expiration.
How much does renewal cost? ▾
$413.00 for a Physician or Administrative Physician license (Compact Physician renewal is also $413.00), per the OPLC Board of Medicine fee page. This includes the mandatory $28.00 Professional Health Program (PHP) fee. Online payment accepts Mastercard, Visa, Discover, and American Express.
Where do I renew? ▾
Through the New Hampshire Online Licensing system at https://forms.nh.gov/license/Login.aspx, via the OPLC Customer Portal. First-time online renewers create an account using the registration code printed on their renewal notification; if you need a code, email CustomerSupport@oplc.nh.gov with your name, telephone number, and license number.
What happens if I miss my renewal deadline? ▾
Your license expires and you are not authorized to practice until it is reinstated; there is no grace period or late-renewal path (RSA 310:8, III). Physician reinstatement costs $413.00 ($378.00 for Compact Physicians). One year after expiration the license lapses, after which you cannot renew and must reinstate by submitting an application and meeting the eligibility requirements in statute and board rules.
Do I have to do anything besides the renewal application and fee? ▾
Yes. Physicians renewing an active-status license must complete the NH Physician Licensure Survey or the opt-out form through DHHS, required by RSA 126-A:5, XVIII-a (administrative and inactive licenses are exempt). Your CME (100 hours per 2-year cycle, at least 40 Category 1, plus 3 hours in pain management/addiction disorders for NH-DEA holders) is now tracked through CE Broker, which OPLC made mandatory for the Board of Medicine as of February 3, 2025.
I hold my NH license through the Interstate Medical Licensure Compact. Is my renewal different? ▾
The board posts a separate document, Important Information for Compact Physician License Renewals, linked from the Applications for Physician page, and the fee schedule lists Compact Physician as its own category (renewal $413.00, reinstatement $378.00). Review that document before renewing rather than assuming the standard path applies.
