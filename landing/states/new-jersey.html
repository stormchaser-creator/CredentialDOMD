i Deadlines, fees, and what happens if you are late

When it is due
Fixed date: all MD and DO licenses expire June 30 of ODD-numbered years, regardless of when the license was issued. The board's licensing presentation states renewal is "Every 2 years on odd years" for MD and DO (example cycle: July 1, 2005 to June 30, 2007), and the DCA's own license verification system shows current MD licenses expiring 6/30/2027. Renewal notices are mailed at least 60 days before expiration (N.J.S.A. 45:1-7.1(e)).
Fee
$580 (active MD/DO, full biennial renewal). The board FAQ states the registration fee is $290 per year of the biennial period; the board's licensing presentation lists renewal fees as: Active $580; Reduced-fee active-retired (65+, no HMO or hospital affiliation) $125; Inactive $0; Retired $0. Dollar figures come from the board's 2017 presentation and the FAQ (last modified 2023); the governing fee rule N.J.A.C. 13:35-6.13 publishes its table only as an image, so the current codified amounts could not be read directly.
Late fee
$100 late fee if renewed within the 30-day window after expiration (board licensing presentation; N.J.A.C. 13:35-3.14 requires "a renewal fee and a late fee" for late renewal).
If you miss the deadline
30-day grace period by statute (N.J.S.A. 45:1-7.1(b)): the license may be renewed within 30 days after expiration by paying the renewal fee plus late fee, and during those 30 days the license remains valid and the physician is not deemed practicing without a license. Any license not renewed within 30 days of expiration is SUSPENDED without a hearing, and continued practice after the 30 days is unlicensed practice even if no suspension notice was received (45:1-7.1(c)). Reinstatement (N.J.S.A. 45:1-7.4; request application via BMEReinstatement@dca.njoag.gov) requires: renewal application, certification of employment for every job held during suspension, the current biennial renewal fee, the unpaid back renewal fee, a $175 reinstatement fee, and proof of the CME that was due (board presentation adds a criminal history background check). A current out-of-state license in good standing with that state's CE satisfied can substitute for the CME proof. The board may require an examination, skills assessment, or refresher course if it suspects practice deficiencies.

1 Step-by-Step: Renewing Your New Jersey Medical License

Follow these steps to renew your medical license with the New Jersey State Board of Medical Examiners (Division of Consumer Affairs):

  1. Complete your CME for the cycle (100 hours) (see details below)
  2. Log in to the New Jersey State Board of Medical Examiners (Division of Consumer Affairs) renewal portal
  3. Complete the renewal application and answer the attestation questions
  4. Pay the renewal fee ($580 (active MD/DO, full biennial renewal))
  5. Submit and save your confirmation

2 New Jersey CME Requirements

100 hours per 2-year cycle. at least 40 Category 1, 2 hrs end-of-life care, 1 hrs opioid prescribing, 2 hrs sexual harassment prevention, 1 hrs implicit bias.

3 Common Pitfalls to Avoid

These are the mistakes New Jersey physicians most commonly make during renewal:

  • Miss June 30 by more than 30 days and the license is suspended automatically, without a hearing and without further notice; practicing after that 30-day window is unlicensed practice of medicine even if you never received a suspension notice (N.J.S.A. 45:1-7.1).
  • Renewal notices go to the address on file and license mail is NOT forwarded by USPS; the board treats failure to report an address change in writing as possible professional misconduct and grounds for discipline (board FAQ).
  • CME extension or waiver requests must reach the board in writing, by certified mail with return receipt, at least 60 days BEFORE the June 30 expiration, and each waiver covers one biennial cycle only (board FAQ).
  • The new 2-credit sexual misconduct prevention CME (N.J.A.C. 13:35-6.15(e), adopted May 2025) must be completed before the close of the renewal in 2027 and must cover all seven board-specified topics; a single generic harassment course may not qualify (board alert on njconsumeraffairs.gov/bme).
  • Reduced-fee ($125) retired-active renewal for physicians 65+ still carries the FULL 100-credit CME requirement, and CME applies whether or not you actually practice in New Jersey; only no-fee Inactive or Retired status (no practice, no prescribing) is CME-exempt (board FAQ).
  • Reinstatement after a renewal lapse is expensive and slow: current cycle fee plus the unpaid back-cycle renewal fee plus $175 reinstatement fee plus $100 late fee, a new criminal history background check, CME verification, and a job-by-job employment certification for the whole suspension period.

4 Frequently Asked Questions

When does my New Jersey medical license expire? ▾
On June 30 of odd-numbered years, for every MD and DO regardless of issue date. The current biennial period ends June 30, 2027. The board mails a renewal notice at least 60 days before expiration.
How much does renewal cost? ▾
$580 for an active MD/DO license ($290 per year of the biennial period). Physicians 65 or older with no HMO or hospital affiliation may renew as reduced-fee active-retired for $125. Renewing as Inactive or Retired is free, but those statuses bar practicing and prescribing in New Jersey.
What happens if I miss the June 30 deadline? ▾
You have a 30-day statutory window: renew with the $580 renewal fee plus a $100 late fee, and your license stays valid during those 30 days. After 30 days the license is suspended automatically without a hearing, and continuing to practice is unlicensed practice even if you never got a notice.
How do I get reinstated after my license was suspended for non-renewal? ▾
Request a reinstatement application by emailing BMEReinstatement@dca.njoag.gov. You must submit the renewal application, a certification listing every job held during the suspension, the current cycle's renewal fee, the unpaid back renewal fee, a $175 reinstatement fee, proof of the required CME, and complete a criminal history background check. Holding a current license in good standing in another state, with that state's CE met, satisfies the CME piece.
Do DOs renew through a different board? ▾
No. New Jersey has one board: the State Board of Medical Examiners licenses both MDs and DOs (and podiatrists) on the same June 30 odd-year cycle, and the board's FAQ confirms DOs hold all the same practice authority as MDs.
Do I have to finish my CME before renewing? ▾
Yes. 100 credits (at least 40 Category 1) for the two years before renewal is a condition of biennial renewal, including the topic mandates such as the 2-credit sexual misconduct prevention course due before the close of the 2027 renewal. Waiver or extension requests must be sent by certified mail at least 60 days before expiration; hours are never prorated for mid-cycle licensure unless you qualify for the new-graduate initial-cycle exemption.
