i Deadlines, fees, and what happens if you are late

When it is due
MD: every other year on or before the licensee's birthday (A.R.S. 32-1430(A); the on-year depends on your license record, check your profile at azmd.gov Doctor Search). DO: December 31 of either even-numbered or odd-numbered years depending on the licensee's assigned renew-by date; the DO renewal portal opens on or about October 1.
Fee
$500 (MD active license, 2-year renewal). DO: $636 biennial, non-refundable.
Late fee
MD: $350 penalty in addition to the $500 fee if renewed more than 30 days after the birthday due date ($850 total on the paper form). DO: $175 late fee in addition to the $636 fee if renewing January 31 through April 30 ($811 total).
If you miss the deadline
MD: renewal within 30 days after the birthday incurs no penalty; from 31 days to 4 months after the birthday you renew with the $350 penalty; the license automatically expires 4 months after the birthday and an expired MD must reapply for licensure (A.R.S. 32-1430(A) and (D)); practicing on an expired license violates the Medical Practice Act. DO: renew by midnight December 31; renewals through January 31 incur no penalty; February 1 through April 30 requires the $175 late fee; the license expires May 1 and the board states there is no reinstatement of an expired DO license, you must reapply as a new physician, which the board says can take up to 6 months with no guarantee of relicensure.
Processing time
MD paper renewals: allow 4-6 weeks (board form instructions); MD online renewals generate an email confirmation when approved, no published timeline. DO online renewal updates the public profile within minutes unless disclosures or status changes require staff review.
MD vs DO
This state runs separate MD and DO requirements. MD: 40 hours per 2-year cycle: 3 hours of opioid prescribing. DO: 40 hours per 2-year cycle: at least 24 hours in Category 1, 3 hours of opioid prescribing.

1 Step-by-Step: Renewing Your Arizona Medical License

Follow these steps to renew your medical license with the Arizona Medical Board:

  1. Complete your CME for the cycle (40 hours) (see details below)
  2. Log in to the Arizona Medical Board renewal portal
  3. Complete the renewal application and answer the attestation questions
  4. Pay the renewal fee ($500 (MD active license, 2-year renewal))
  5. Submit and save your confirmation

2 Arizona CME Requirements

3 Common Pitfalls to Avoid

These are the mistakes Arizona physicians most commonly make during renewal:

  • MD and DO run on completely different clocks in Arizona. MDs renew on or before their birthday every other year; DOs renew by December 31 of their assigned even or odd year. A dual-boarded practice manager who assumes one deadline will miss the other.
  • An expired Arizona DO license has no reinstatement path at all. After May 1 the board requires a full new-physician application, quotes up to 6 months to process, and states there is no guarantee of relicensure. The MD board likewise requires reapplication once the license auto-expires 4 months after the birthday.
  • DO renewal is online only: no paper form exists, checks are not accepted, and credit card payments are not taken by phone. The DO portal takes Visa, MasterCard and Discover; the MD online renewal takes Visa, MasterCard and AMEX (no Discover). Bring the right card for the right board.
  • Any Arizona MD with a DEA registration (or intending to get one) must register with the Controlled Substances Prescription Monitoring Program (CSPMP) under A.R.S. 36-2606; the board warns failure to register may result in Board action.
  • DO licensees should enter all CME in the Licensee Portal before starting the renewal, and a dual-purpose course (AOA 1-A plus opioid CME) must be entered twice, once under each requirement. DO CME waiver and extension requests are only accepted October 1 through January 30, with an approved extension giving until April 30.

4 Frequently Asked Questions

When is my Arizona MD license renewal due? ▾
Every other year on or before your birthday (A.R.S. 32-1430(A)). You can renew up to 30 days after your birthday without penalty; after that a $350 late penalty applies, and the license automatically expires 4 months after your birthday.
How much does renewal cost? ▾
MD: $500 for the 2-year renewal, plus a $350 penalty if postmarked 31 or more days after the due date. DO: $636 biennial, plus a $175 late fee if renewing January 31 through April 30; all DO fees are non-refundable.
What happens if I let my license lapse? ▾
MD: the license automatically expires 4 months after your birthday and you must reapply for licensure; practicing after expiration violates the Medical Practice Act. DO: the license expires May 1 with no reinstatement; you must reapply as a new physician, which the board says can take up to 6 months with no guarantee of relicensure.
Can I renew on paper? ▾
MD: yes, the board publishes an MD Paper Renewal form and asks you to allow 4-6 weeks for processing, though online renewal is preferred. DO: no, the process is entirely online through the Thentia licensee portal and the board will not process mailed checks.
Do I need to submit CME certificates with my renewal? ▾
Only if selected for audit. MDs attest to 40 hours per cycle and submit the CME Audit Form with proof (including the 3 hours of opioid prescribing CME) only when audited. DOs attest and are also subject to random audit; the DO board holds the physician responsible for recordkeeping and lets you log CME in the Licensee Portal.
When does the DO renewal window open? ▾
The DO renewal portal opens on or about October 1 of the renewal year, and renewal notices are mailed the last Friday in October. Renew by December 31 to avoid issues; January 1-31 is a no-penalty grace period.
