i Deadlines, fees, and what happens if you are late

When it is due
Licenses expire September 30 in alternating years by last name: A-L expire in even years (renewal window July 15 to September 30, 2026, 11:59 pm EST), M-Z in odd years. Not tied to birth month.
Fee
$512.00 total for MDs and DOs (2026 cycle): $436 renewal fee + $50 rehabilitation/peer review program + $26 Maryland Health Care Commission assessment (Health Occ. sec. 1-209). IMLC compact-licensed physicians pay an additional $25 Compact processing fee ($537 total, paid to the Compact).
If you miss the deadline
No grace period and no late-renewal window. The online renewal system closes at 11:59 pm EST on September 30 and is not available afterward. An unrenewed license expires; practicing on an expired license risks a civil fine of up to $50,000 (Health Occ. secs. 14-601, 14-606). The only path back is the online Physician Reinstatement application: $700 nonrefundable fee, a new criminal history records check, and 50 Category I or II CME credits (at least 25 Category I) in the 2 years preceding the reinstatement application. Revoked, surrendered, or suspended licenses cannot use the online reinstatement system.
Processing time
Completed renewal applications are processed the next business day; the public website reflects the new expiration date 1 to 2 business days after processing. A digital copy of the renewed license is printable immediately after payment.

1 Step-by-Step: Renewing Your Maryland Medical License

Follow these steps to renew your medical license with the Maryland Board of Physicians:

  1. Complete your CME for the cycle (50 hours) (see details below)
  2. Log in to the Maryland Board of Physicians renewal portal
  3. Complete the renewal application and answer the attestation questions
  4. Pay the renewal fee ($512)
  5. Submit and save your confirmation

2 Maryland CME Requirements

50 hours per 2-year cycle.

  • At least 25 hours in Category 1
  • 2 hours of controlled substances
  • Required topics: Implicit Bias

3 Common Pitfalls to Avoid

These are the mistakes Maryland physicians most commonly make during renewal:

  • There is no grace period whatsoever. The online application shuts off at 11:59 pm EST on September 30 and cannot be accessed afterward; missing it means a $700 reinstatement fee plus a brand-new criminal history records check before you can practice again.
  • An unresolved tax liability with the Maryland Comptroller's Office blocks access to the renewal application entirely. The Board cannot help; you must clear it with the Comptroller (410-974-2432) and wait for official notification to the Board before you can log in.
  • Renewal years alternate by last name (A-L in even years, M-Z in odd years), all expiring September 30. Physicians who moved from a birth-month state sometimes assume the wrong anchor.
  • First renewal after initial licensure requires completing the New Physician Orientation (NPO) program BEFORE starting the renewal application. Separately, anyone renewing for the first time after April 1, 2026 must attest to one-time structural racism training, and implicit bias training is required once if never previously attested.
  • Payment is by Visa, MasterCard, or Discover only; American Express is not listed. Also have CDS, DEA, and NPI numbers and workers' comp info ready, since the application asks for them.
  • If selected for CME audit and your credits are not in ACCME's PARS system, documentation must reach the Board by October 15, 2026 (two weeks after the renewal deadline).

4 Frequently Asked Questions

When does my Maryland medical license expire and when can I renew? ▾
Licenses expire September 30 on a 2-year cycle that alternates by last name: A-L in even years, M-Z in odd years. For the 2026 cycle (last names A-L), the online renewal window opened July 15, 2026 and closes at 11:59 pm EST on September 30, 2026.
How much does renewal cost? ▾
$512.00 for both MDs and DOs: $436 renewal fee, $50 rehabilitation/peer review program, and a $26 Maryland Health Care Commission assessment required by Health Occ. sec. 1-209. Physicians renewing through the Interstate Medical Licensure Compact pay a $25 Compact processing fee on top ($537 total).
Is there a late fee or grace period if I miss September 30? ▾
No. There is no late fee because there is no late renewal: the online system closes at 11:59 pm EST on September 30 and the license expires. To practice again you must reinstate: $700 fee, a new criminal history records check, and 50 Category I or II CME credits (25+ Category I) earned in the 2 years before applying.
What do I need on hand to complete the online renewal? ▾
Your Maryland license number, date of birth and last 4 of your SSN, a Visa/MasterCard/Discover card, CDS and DEA registration numbers and NPI if applicable, workers' compensation coverage information if applicable, and completion of the New Physician Orientation program if this is your first renewal after initial licensure.
Do I have to send CME certificates with my renewal? ▾
No, renewal is by attestation (50 Category I or II credits in the 2 years preceding expiration, at least 25 Category I; waived at your first renewal after initial licensure). The Board may audit you. If your credits were reported to ACCME's PARS, the Board verifies them there automatically; otherwise, audited physicians must send documentation by October 15, 2026. Keep CME records for 6 years.
How fast is the renewal processed and when do I get my license? ▾
You can download and print a digital copy of the renewed license and receipt immediately after payment. Completed applications are processed the next business day, and the Board's website shows the new expiration date within 1 to 2 business days.
