AI Document Scanner

AI Assistant

CME Tracking & Review

Licenses in One State or Many

Renewal Reminders

Health Records & Screenings

CV & Credential Exports

Optional Practice tools

Manage the business side when you need it.

Add contracts, work logs, invoices and payment tracking to your credential record. Practice is an optional addition for physicians managing independent work or locum assignments.

AI RVU Coder

Dictate what you did ("C3-4 posterior laminectomy as the assistant") and get CPT codes with work RVUs, add-on levels, and modifiers from a CMS-grounded catalog. Your case log builds itself.

Example: a one-level TLIF dictation returns the fusion, cage, and instrumentation codes with the assistant modifier pre-selected.

Contract Scanner

Upload a locum agreement and the AI extracts your rates, call stipend, coverage dates, and billing terms straight off the PDF, then your work logs price themselves against those terms.

Example: "$3,000 per 24-hour call covering the first 4 hours, $300/hr after" is read from the agreement and applied to every entry.

One-Tap Call Logging

Phone rings, tap the timer. Calls, rounding, procedures, and consults log themselves, round to your contract's billing increment, and draw down your stipend hours in real time.

Example: a 3-minute call back at 2am logs as a 15-minute billed block and lands on the right call day, automatically.

Invoices That Match Your Contract

Professional PDF invoices with one daily total per call day and every piece of work itemized beneath it. Track each invoice from sent to paid, with aging on what is outstanding.

Example: six days of call becomes a clean one-page PDF: stipends, overage hours, and orientation, each priced by your contract's terms.

RVU Capture by Voice

Say what you did in plain language. The AI codes it against official CMS values (procedures, consults, progress notes) and keeps daily, monthly, and all-time wRVU totals.

Example: "placed an EVD and saw two consults" comes back coded with CPT codes and work RVUs, ready to save.

For Locum Tenens Physicians

The agency's portal is the agency's ledger. This one is yours.

Keep your own contract terms, work log, invoices and payments across agencies. Review the scanner’s extracted rates, then use them to price your work. Your license and CME deadlines stay alongside the assignments they support.

  • Upload your agreement, get a billing engine. Stipends, hourly rates, coverage dates, and billing increments are read off the contract and applied to every entry you log.
  • Stipend math handled for you. Call days count logged work down against your covered hours; anything beyond bills at your after-stipend rate. A live countdown shows where you stand all day.
  • Invoices a hospital biller respects. Daily totals with the work itemized beneath, quarter-hour billing blocks, orientation at its own rate, exported as a clean PDF and tracked sent to paid.
  • Check what was billed against what was paid. Record full or partial payments against each invoice and see the remaining balance. Use the itemized work log when you follow up with the agency.
  • Travel invoiced with receipts attached. Photo the hotel bill and the rental receipt; one tap sends the agency an expense invoice with documentation, tracked to reimbursement.
  • See the year before it happens. A billing forecast calendar projects income from your real per-day averages, and multistate tax prep estimates what you owe each state, tracks quarterly payments, and turns card statements into a deduction ledger. Review the supported state models with your tax preparer.

Your records and uploaded documents are stored with Supabase. Account access rules are designed to separate users’ records, and the operator has administrative access for support and running the service. AI features send the content you submit to Google or Anthropic; shared service keys are available, and your own key is optional. You keep working. The app is offline first: your records live in your own browser as well as on the server, so a flight, a hospital basement or an outage at our end does not stop you opening a licence number or logging a case. Changes queue on the device and sync when the connection returns.

Behind that there are three separate copies. The database is Postgres at Supabase in the United States, which takes its own daily snapshot. Every night at 03:10 a second job runs on hardware we control and pulls a full copy: every table, the schema, and every document you have uploaded, into one archive encrypted with AES-256 before it is written to disk. That archive is kept for 30 days locally and copied off site for 14. Both copies carry a checksum so a corrupted file is detected rather than discovered during a restore.

The reason the nightly job exists is specific: the provider’s own snapshots cover the database but not your uploaded files. Without it, a lost project would mean lost documents. With it, the documents sit in a second place, in our custody, encrypted.
Tested. A backup nobody has restored is a hope, not a failsafe. The archive has been decrypted and loaded into a clean Postgres database end to end, table counts and row hashes compared against the live database, and a document opened from the restored copy. That result is written down, and the runbook puts the next full restore test on the calendar quarterly.

An archive can also be opened without us and without Supabase: it is a plain tar file behind standard OpenSSL encryption, holding your data as JSON and CSV and your documents as the original files. There is no proprietary format to be locked into and no tool of ours you need in order to read it.
