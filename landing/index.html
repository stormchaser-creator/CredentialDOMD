Why I built CredentialDOMD

“I needed a better answer than ‘somewhere on my computer.’”

During training, I nearly missed a deadline that could have cost me my license and possibly my career. I needed a dependable way to see what was due and keep the paperwork I would be asked to produce for years, sometimes decades.

Then came hospital privileges, state licenses and credentialing applications. Each one asked for an extraordinary amount of documentation. Often, it was the same documentation I had already supplied somewhere else.

When I ask colleagues where they keep their records, the answer I hear most often is, “Somewhere on my computer.” That was my problem, too. Having a file somewhere didn’t mean I could find it when I needed it, or know whether it was the right version.

So I started with a simple idea: give every document a home. Take a picture, upload a file or forward an email. Let the app read and organize it, review the details, and keep it ready for the next request.

Today, CredentialDOMD holds the records I’ve been asked for throughout my own credentialing across multiple states and hospitals. Nurse practitioners and physician assistants face the same requests, and they deserve a dependable home for that paperwork, too.

When someone asks, I have it. That’s the peace of mind I wanted to share.

DOs, MDs, NPs and PAs. It tracks licenses and certifications for all four.

What if it doesn't make my life easier?

Ask for a full refund of your most recent annual payment, including a renewal, at any time. No deadline and no prorating.

How do I get my paperwork in?

Take a photo, upload a file, or forward an email. You can also start from your CV. The app reads supported documents and files the details; you review them and fix anything that needs it.

Does it renew my license or do credentialing for me?

No. It keeps your records organized and shows what is coming due. You still complete board renewals, hospital applications and attestations yourself. It is not primary source verification.

Can I use it on my phone?

Yes. It works on your phone and your computer, and you can add it to your home screen.

I signed up at credentialfile.app. Is that a different account?

No. CredentialFile was an earlier name for the same app. Log in at credentialdomd.com/app with the same account and your records are there.

Can my group use it?

Not yet. Group features are on the roadmap. Email support@credentialdomd.com to tell us what your group needs.
