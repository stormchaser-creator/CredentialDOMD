Everything You Need

Every credential. One app. Zero surprises.

CredentialDOMD replaces your spreadsheet, your sticky notes, your calendar reminders, that folder of scanned PDFs you can never find — and for locums, your entire billing workflow.

AI Document Scanner

Snap a photo of any license, DEA card, board certificate, insurance COI, or vaccine card. The AI reads it, pulls every field, and files it in the right place for your one-tap approval.

Example: a photo of your state license files itself — number, issue date, expiration — in about ten seconds.

AI Assistant

Chat with your own file. Ask what is expiring, hand it a document that fits no standard form (nothing gets dropped), or paste an agency's request. Nothing changes without your approval.

Example: paste a credentialing checklist — it bundles the nine documents you have into one send and lists exactly what is missing.

CME Compliance Engine

Cycle-aware CME tracking anchored to each license's renewal date, with the real state rules built in: category minimums, topic mandates, MD vs DO requirements, and the DEA MATE Act.

Example: Colorado's 30-hour cycle with its 2-hour substance-use mandate, counted down against your actual renewal date.

Multi-State License Matrix

Hold licenses in three states? One view shows every license, DEA registration, renewal date, and CME standing side by side — with each state's rules applied independently.

Example: adding a new state license automatically starts tracking that state's CME requirements on your Home screen.

Alerts That Escalate

Expiration alerts sharpen as deadlines approach, across licenses, certifications, TB tests, and CME cycles. One tap builds a renewal packet: transcript plus certificates, ready to share.

Example: a record saved without an expiration date gets flagged immediately — undated credentials are how lapses happen.

Health Records & Screenings

Titers with lab values and reference ranges, TB tests, drug screens with chain of custody, vaccine series, and background screenings with per-search status — everything agencies ask for.

Example: a measles titer that comes back non-immune is flagged the day the report lands, not the week an assignment needs it.

Contract Scanner

Upload a locum agreement and the AI extracts your rates, call stipend, coverage dates, and billing terms straight off the PDF — then your work logs price themselves against those terms.

Example: "$3,000 per 24-hour call covering the first 4 hours, $300/hr after" — read from the agreement, applied to every entry.

One-Tap Call Logging

Phone rings, tap the timer. Calls, rounding, procedures, and consults log themselves, round to your contract's billing increment, and draw down your stipend hours in real time.

Example: a 3-minute call back at 2am logs as a 15-minute billed block and lands on the right call day — automatically.

Invoices That Match Your Contract

Professional PDF invoices with one daily total per call day and every piece of work itemized beneath it. Track each invoice from sent to paid, with aging on what is outstanding.

Example: six days of call becomes a clean one-page PDF — stipends, overage hours, and orientation, each priced by your contract's terms.

RVU Capture by Voice

Say what you did in plain language. The AI codes it against official CMS values — procedures, consults, progress notes — and keeps daily, monthly, and all-time wRVU totals.

Example: "placed an EVD and saw two consults" comes back coded with CPT codes and work RVUs, ready to save.

CV, Exports & 1099 Deductions

Generate a formatted CV from your credential data. Export everything as CSV or PDF. A deduction ledger auto-collects your licensing and credentialing costs for tax season.

Example: license fees, DEA renewals, and CME costs flow into a Schedule C-ready export for your CPA.

Offline, Private, Yours

Installs on your phone like a native app and works with zero signal — then syncs across devices when you are back online. Row-level security means your data is isolated to your account.

Example: pull up your license number in a hospital basement with no reception. It is all still there.
