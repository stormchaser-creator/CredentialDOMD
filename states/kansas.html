Kansas (KS)

Kansas Medical License Renewal, 2026

Annual (1 year) renewal through the Kansas State Board of Healing Arts (KSBHA). All MD licenses expire July 31 and all DO licenses expire October 31, every year. Verified from the board's own pages, August 2026.

i Deadlines, fees, and what happens if you are late

When it is due
Fixed calendar dates by license type, not birth month. MD: all Medical Doctor licenses expire July 31; the renewal cycle runs May 15 through July 31 each year, with on-time renewal due by June 30 and July 1 to July 31 as the late-renewal window. DO: all Osteopathic Doctor licenses expire October 31; renewal cycle August 15 through October 31, on-time by September 30, late window October 1 to October 31.
Fee
$360 online ($430 by paper) for annual renewal of an active or federally active MD/DO license under the Healing Arts Act; inactive or exempt license renews at $150; resident active $100; reentry active $300
Late fee
Additional +$100 for online late renewal (+$230 for paper late renewal) of an active or federally active license; inactive/exempt late renewal adds +$55 online (+$80 paper)
If you miss the deadline
There is a built-in late month with a late fee (MD: July 1-31; DO: October 1-31). If not renewed by the end of that month, the license goes to "Cancelled - Failure to Renew" status the next day (MD: August 1; DO: November 1). Practicing on a cancelled license may subject the physician to disciplinary action. Reinstatement of a cancelled license requires a reinstatement application and a $400 fee, and the board may require additional testing, training, or education depending on the circumstances (reinstatement of a revoked license is $1,000).

1 Step-by-Step: Renewing Your Kansas Medical License

Follow these steps to renew your medical license with the Kansas State Board of Healing Arts (KSBHA):

  1. Complete your CME for the cycle (50 hours) (see details below)
  2. Log in to the Kansas State Board of Healing Arts (KSBHA) renewal portal
  3. Complete the renewal application and answer the attestation questions
  4. Pay the renewal fee ($360 online ($430 by paper) for annual renewal of an active or federally active MD/DO license under the Healing Arts Act; inactive or exempt license renews at $150; resident active $100; reentry active $300)
  5. Submit and save your confirmation

2 Kansas CME Requirements

50 hours per year, including at least 20 Category 1, 3 hours opioid prescribing.

3 Common Pitfalls to Avoid

These are the mistakes Kansas physicians most commonly make during renewal:

  • The printed expiration date is NOT the on-time deadline. An MD license expires July 31, but on-time renewal ends June 30; renewing in July costs the late fee (+$100 online, +$230 paper). Same structure for DOs: expires October 31 but on-time renewal ends September 30.
  • MD and DO renew on different calendars at the same board: MD May 15 to July 31, DO August 15 to October 31. Dual-degree groups and credentialing staff tracking both types miss this split.
  • Paper renewal is materially more expensive: $430 vs $360 on time, and the paper late surcharge is $230 vs $100 online, so a paper late renewal totals $660 vs $460 online.
  • One day past the late window the license is administratively cancelled (status Cancelled - Failure to Renew on August 1 for MD, November 1 for DO). Reinstatement is $400, may require additional testing, training, or education at the board's discretion, and practicing on a cancelled license can draw disciplinary action.
  • An active Kansas license carries a mandatory malpractice insurance condition: the Health Care Stabilization Fund Act requires coverage of at least $500,000 per claim / $1,500,000 annual aggregate (K.S.A. 40-3402, 40-3404, 65-2809(c)). Physicians who leave practice but keep an active license at renewal still carry this obligation; exempt or inactive designations avoid it.

4 Frequently Asked Questions

When is my Kansas medical license renewal due? ▾
MD licenses all expire July 31 and renew annually; the renewal window opens May 15 and on-time renewal ends June 30, with July 1-31 as the late window. DO licenses all expire October 31; the window opens August 15, on-time renewal ends September 30, and October 1-31 is the late window. Kansas does not use birth-month renewal for physicians.
How much does renewal cost? ▾
$360 for online renewal of an active or federally active MD/DO license, $430 by paper. Late renewal adds $100 online or $230 by paper. Inactive and exempt licenses renew at $150.
How do I renew online? ▾
Log in to the KSBHA Online Portal at https://boha.ks.gov/egov_boha/Login.aspx during your renewal cycle. The portal also provides your wallet card (emailed after issuance and accessible anytime) and address updates. The board accepts checks, money orders, and Debit/Visa/MasterCard/Discover/American Express.
What happens if I miss the final deadline? ▾
On the day after the late window closes (August 1 for MDs, November 1 for DOs), the license status becomes Cancelled - Failure to Renew. Practicing after cancellation may subject you to disciplinary action. Reinstatement requires an application and a $400 fee, and the board may require additional testing, training, or education depending on the circumstances.
Can I switch to inactive or exempt status instead of paying the active fee? ▾
Yes. A status change can be requested at renewal, or outside the renewal cycle via the Designation/Type Change Form. Inactive and exempt licenses renew at $150. Converting back from inactive or exempt to active costs $175.
Do I need malpractice insurance to hold an active Kansas license? ▾
Yes. The Kansas Health Care Stabilization Fund Act requires physicians with an active Kansas license to maintain professional liability coverage of at least $500,000 per claim and $1,500,000 annual aggregate (K.S.A. 40-3402, 40-3404, 65-2809(c)).
