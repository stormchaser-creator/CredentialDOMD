i Deadlines, fees, and what happens if you are late

When it is due
MD: annual reregistration during the month of initial licensure, per OAC 435:10-7-10 ("Reregistration shall be conducted during the month of initial licensure of each individual licensee"). It is NOT birth month and NOT a fixed calendar date. DO: all osteopathic licenses expire June 30 every year; the OSBOE renewal window opens May 1.
Fee
MD: $200 (application for annual reregistration fee, full medical license, OAC 435:1-1-7). DO: $250 annually (OSBOE FAQ and fee rule OAC 510:10-7).
Late fee
MD: the board FAQ states any renewal postmarked after expiration is late and "the late fee will be required," but the current fee rule (OAC 435:1-1-7) publishes no separate MD late-fee amount; the published lapse-path fees are a $350 reactivation processing fee and $500 reinstatement of license. DO: $150 late re-registration fee, charged for renewals July 1 through August 31 (OAC 510:10-7; OSBOE FAQ).
If you miss the deadline
MD: no grace period ("No grace period beyond that provided by law shall be allowed," OAC 435:10-7-10). A license inactive more than 60 days requires the full reinstatement process (board licensing FAQ, reinstatement fee $500). By statute 59 O.S. 495d, failure to reregister within 60 days after the registration period ends suspends the license, the Board notifies the district attorney, and reinstatement requires meeting current initial-licensure standards plus possible clinical competency examination unless at least 50 percent of monthly activity during the lapse was medical practice. Practicing without a current renewal is a misdemeanor with a fine of at least $1,000 and grounds for revocation (59 O.S. 495b). DO: renewal with $150 late fee allowed July 1 to August 31; after cancellation a $500 reinstatement fee applies (within 2 years of cancellation) in addition to other fees.
Processing time
Per the MD board licensing FAQ (written for the legacy process): renewals are posted daily as received and wallet cards are printed and mailed each Monday for everyone who renewed the previous week; initial license applications average 6 to 8 weeks. No processing-time commitment is published for the new 2026 portal.
MD vs DO
This state runs separate MD and DO requirements. MD: 60 hours per 3-year cycle: at least 60 hours in Category 1, 1 hour of opioid prescribing. DO: 16 hours per year: at least 16 hours in Category 1, 1 hour of controlled substances.

1 Step-by-Step: Renewing Your Oklahoma Medical License

Follow these steps to renew your medical license with the Oklahoma State Board of Medical Licensure and Supervision:

  1. Complete your CME for the cycle (60 hours) (see details below)
  2. Log in to the Oklahoma State Board of Medical Licensure and Supervision renewal portal
  3. Complete the renewal application and answer the attestation questions
  4. Pay the renewal fee (MD: $200 (application for annual reregistration fee, full medical license, OAC 435:1-1-7))
  5. Submit and save your confirmation

2 Oklahoma CME Requirements

MD: 60 hours per 3-year cycle.

  • At least 60 hours in Category 1
  • 1 hour of opioid prescribing

DO: 16 hours per year.

  • At least 16 hours in Category 1

3 Common Pitfalls to Avoid

These are the mistakes Oklahoma physicians most commonly make during renewal:

  • Your MD renewal month is the month you were FIRST licensed in Oklahoma (OAC 435:10-7-10), not your birth month and not a calendar-year date; physicians moving from birth-month states routinely mark the wrong month.
  • Not receiving the renewal notice does not save you: the board mails notices about 60 days out as a courtesy only, and its FAQ says outright that a renewal postmarked after expiration is late and that not receiving a notice is not grounds for waiving the late fee.
  • The 60-day cliff is steep: a license inactive more than 60 days requires full reinstatement ($500, Board Secretary or Board review, current initial-licensure standards, and possibly a clinical competency exam under 59 O.S. 495d), not a simple late renewal.
  • The MD board migrated to a brand-new licensing portal (medicalboard.ok.gov) in 2026 and, as of August 19, 2026, its renewal and bill-pay services were suspended for planned maintenance downtime; the legacy pay.apps.ok.gov/medlic/md portal now just redirects. Do not wait until the last days of your renewal month.
  • DOs are on a completely different clock and board: OSBOE licenses all expire June 30 every year (renewal opens May 1, late window with $150 fee runs only to August 31, then cancellation), and CME is verified through CE Broker at renewal.
  • Practicing on an expired Oklahoma MD renewal is a misdemeanor carrying a fine of not less than $1,000 and is grounds for suspension or revocation (59 O.S. 495b).

4 Frequently Asked Questions

When is my Oklahoma MD license renewal due? ▾
Every year, during the month of your initial Oklahoma licensure. OAC 435:10-7-10 requires annual reregistration conducted during the month of initial licensure, and allows no grace period beyond what statute provides.
How much does MD renewal cost? ▾
$200 per year for a full medical license (application for annual reregistration fee, OAC 435:1-1-7). A November 2025 proposed rule amendment states the board is simplifying the fee structure and not adding any new fees.
What happens if I miss my renewal? ▾
The renewal is late the moment it is postmarked after expiration and a late charge applies. If the license stays inactive more than 60 days you must go through full reinstatement: $500 fee, review by the Board Secretary or full Board, current initial-licensure standards, and possibly a clinical competency examination (board FAQ; 59 O.S. 495d). The fee schedule also lists a $350 reactivation processing fee for the shorter-lapse path.
Where do I renew online? ▾
Through the board's new licensing portal at https://www.medicalboard.ok.gov/s/, which handles renewal, CME posting, profile updates, and wallet card download. The old pay.apps.ok.gov/medlic/md portal permanently redirects there. Note: as of August 19, 2026 the board's site showed renewal and bill-pay services temporarily suspended for planned maintenance.
Do I have to report CME at every renewal? ▾
You certify at renewal that you hold at least 60 AMA PRA Category 1 hours within the preceding three years; the certification cycle is triennial even though the renewal itself is annual. Newly licensed and reinstated physicians begin reporting three years from licensure or reinstatement (board licensing FAQ). DEA registrants also need 1 hour per year on pain management or opioid use per the board's CME guidelines.
I am a DO. Is my renewal different? ▾
Yes, entirely. DOs renew with the Oklahoma State Board of Osteopathic Examiners: every license expires June 30, renewal opens May 1, the fee is $250, a $150 late fee applies July 1 to August 31, and a cancelled license costs $500 to reinstate. Renewal is done in the OSBOE Thentia portal (osboe.portalus.thentiacloud.net) and CME (16 hours per year) is verified via CE Broker.
