i Deadlines, fees, and what happens if you are late

When it is due
Fixed dates in even-numbered years, and the two boards differ: MD licenses expire December 31 of every even-numbered year (except MD Training licenses); DO licenses expire October 31 of every even-numbered year. Current cycle ends 10/31/2026 (DO) and 12/31/2026 (MD). Renewal opens in PALS approximately 60 days before expiration.
Fee
$360 (MD unrestricted license, 2-year renewal, 49 Pa. Code 16.13); $450 (DO unrestricted license, 2-year renewal, 49 Pa. Code 25.231, rate effective for the Nov 1, 2024 to Oct 31, 2026 biennium and thereafter). IMLC compact renewals add a $25.00 IMLC fee paid through imlcc.com.
Late fee
$5 for each month or part of a month past the renewal date (BPOA late-renewal penalty; stated for MDs in 49 Pa. Code 16.15 and for DOs in the 25.231 fee schedule as "Penalty for late biennial renewal, per month or part of month: $5")
If you miss the deadline
No grace period. Failure to pay the biennial renewal fee on time "automatically causes the license... to be placed in an unregistered status," and practice is prohibited while unregistered (49 Pa. Code 16.15). Practicing after expiration incurs the $5/month penalty and can bring disciplinary action or criminal prosecution. Reactivation to active status requires paying current and back registration plus penalty fees, a notarized affidavit covering the non-practice period, a resume of activities, and at least 2 hours of approved child abuse recognition and reporting training. The 2026 BPOA compact renewal guide confirms that missing the deadline means the license expires and a reactivation application is required.
MD vs DO
This state runs separate MD and DO requirements. MD: 100 hours per 2-year cycle. at least 20 Category 1, 12 hrs patient safety, 2 hrs child abuse recognition, 2 hrs pain management, 2 hrs organ and tissue donation. DO: 100 hours per 2-year cycle. at least 20 Category 1, 12 hrs patient safety, 2 hrs child abuse recognition, 2 hrs pain management, 2 hrs organ and tissue donation.

1 Step-by-Step: Renewing Your Pennsylvania Medical License

Follow these steps to renew your medical license with the Pennsylvania State Board of Medicine (Bureau of Professional and Occupational Affairs, Department of State):

  1. Complete your CME for the cycle (100 hours) (see details below)
  2. Log in to the Pennsylvania State Board of Medicine (Bureau of Professional and Occupational Affairs, Department of State) renewal portal
  3. Complete the renewal application and answer the attestation questions
  4. Pay the renewal fee ($360 (MD unrestricted license, 2-year renewal, 49 Pa)
  5. Submit and save your confirmation

2 Pennsylvania CME Requirements

MD: 100 hours per 2-year cycle. at least 20 Category 1, 12 hrs patient safety, 2 hrs child abuse recognition, 2 hrs pain management, 2 hrs organ and tissue donation. DO: 100 hours per 2-year cycle. at least 20 Category 1, 12 hrs patient safety, 2 hrs child abuse recognition, 2 hrs pain management, 2 hrs organ and tissue donation.

3 Common Pitfalls to Avoid

These are the mistakes Pennsylvania physicians most commonly make during renewal:

  • DOs expire two months before MDs: the osteopathic license expires October 31 of even years while the MD license expires December 31. A DO who assumes the familiar December 31 date is already two months lapsed (pa.gov osteopathic renewal page; pa.gov medicine renewal page).
  • Do not upload your child abuse CE certificate to the renewal application. The 2-hour child abuse recognition and reporting course must come from a Board-approved provider, and verification must be received electronically from the course provider; an uploaded certificate does not satisfy the requirement (BPOA 2026 compact renewal guide, pa.gov).
  • IMLC compact licensees must renew in the right order: submit the Pennsylvania renewal in PALS first, then the compact renewal at imlcc.com. The Board's own guide warns that doing it out of order may delay the renewal, and the Board cannot give status updates on compact applications (BPOA 2026 compact renewal guide).
  • There is no grace period. On November 1 (DO) or January 1 (MD) an unrenewed license goes to unregistered status automatically, practice is prohibited, a $5/month penalty accrues for practice past the date, and returning requires a reactivation application with back fees and a notarized non-practice affidavit (49 Pa. Code 16.15).
  • Renewal notices go to the address and email on file: PALS emails you when renewal opens about 60 days out, and the Board mails notices 2 to 3 months before expiration to the most recent address reported. Stale contact info means no warning, and CME certificates are audit-only, so keep proof rather than uploading it (pa.gov renewal pages; BPOA 2026 compact renewal guide).

4 Frequently Asked Questions

When does my Pennsylvania medical license expire? ▾
MD licenses expire December 31 of every even-numbered year (except MD Training licenses); DO licenses expire October 31 of every even-numbered year. The current cycle ends 10/31/2026 for DOs and 12/31/2026 for MDs.
How do I renew, and when can I start? ▾
Renewal is online only at www.pals.pa.gov; there are no paper renewal applications. Renewals open approximately 60 days before the expiration date, and PALS emails you when your renewal is available. The boards also mail notices 2 to 3 months before expiration.
How much does renewal cost? ▾
$360 for an unrestricted MD license and $450 for an unrestricted DO license, each covering the 2-year cycle. IMLC compact licensees pay an additional $25.00 IMLC renewal fee through imlcc.com.
What happens if I miss the deadline? ▾
The license automatically drops to unregistered status and you may not practice. Practicing past the date adds a $5 penalty per month or part of a month and exposes you to discipline or criminal prosecution. Returning requires a reactivation application with current and back fees, a notarized affidavit for the non-practice period, and 2 hours of child abuse recognition CE.
Do I upload my CME certificates with the renewal? ▾
No. You attest to completing the 100 hours; do not upload certificates. The Board audits after the renewal period and proof is only required if you are audited. The exception process for child abuse CE is stricter: the approved provider must report your completion electronically to the Board.
I hold my Pennsylvania license through the IMLC. Anything different? ▾
Yes, it is a two-step process: first submit the Pennsylvania renewal in PALS, then submit the compact renewal at imlcc.com (where the $360 or $450 state fee plus the $25 IMLC fee is paid). Renewing out of order can delay your renewal, and compact application status questions go to the IMLC, not the Board.
