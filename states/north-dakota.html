i Deadlines, fees, and what happens if you are late

When it is due
Licenses expire on the physician's birthday every other year (effective August 1, 2023). A new license first expires the second time the birthday comes around after Board approval. The online renewal window opens 59 days before the expiration date; email reminders go out 59 and 14 days before expiration.
Fee
$405.00 online renewal for a two-year license (board renewal page, effective August 1, 2023). Underlying rule sets licensure at $200 per year (N.D. Admin. Code 50-02-07.1-01); the extra $5 appears to be an online processing charge, which the board does not itemize.
Late fee
No separate late penalty within 3 years of expiration; instead, arrearage of $200 per year for each year past the renewal deadline, up to three years (N.D. Admin. Code 50-02-07.1-02.1). A $500 unlicensed-practice fee applies if the physician practiced in ND after expiration (50-02-07.1-02).
If you miss the deadline
No grace period for practice: the license is expired on the birthday deadline. An expired license can be renewed through the License Dashboard for up to 3 years after expiration by answering whether the physician practiced in-state since lapsing and paying renewal fees in arrearage ($200/year). After 3 years, a complete new application is required, though prior medical school and postgraduate training verifications do not have to be resubmitted. If found to have practiced after expiration, a $500 unlicensed-practice fee is assessed in addition to other penalties.

1 Step-by-Step: Renewing Your North Dakota Medical License

Follow these steps to renew your medical license with the North Dakota Board of Medicine:

  1. Complete your CME for the cycle (40 hours) (see details below)
  2. Log in to the North Dakota Board of Medicine renewal portal
  3. Complete the renewal application and answer the attestation questions
  4. Pay the renewal fee ($405)
  5. Submit and save your confirmation

3 Common Pitfalls to Avoid

These are the mistakes North Dakota physicians most commonly make during renewal:

  • The expiration anchor changed on August 1, 2023 to the physician's birthday every other year, so a calendar reminder built on the old renewal date will be wrong; your first renewal falls on the second birthday after Board approval, which can make the first cycle shorter or longer than 24 months.
  • The portal will not let you renew more than 59 days before expiration; the board's own instructions note that an 'ineligible for renewal' error usually means you are outside the 59-day window, not that something is wrong with your license.
  • There is no practice grace period: working even briefly in North Dakota on an expired license triggers a $500 unlicensed-practice fee under N.D. Admin. Code 50-02-07.1-02, on top of the arrearage owed to reinstate.
  • Let the license sit expired past 3 years and you must file a complete new application (only medical school and training verifications are waived), instead of the simple dashboard arrearage renewal available inside 3 years.
  • The renewal includes a CME compliance certification; a false certification carries a $500 administrative sanction under N.D. Admin. Code 50-02-07.1-03, and the board audits renewing physicians, so keep CME records for at least one year after reporting.

4 Frequently Asked Questions

When does my North Dakota medical license expire? ▾
On your birthday, every other year (effective August 1, 2023). A newly issued license first expires the second time your birthday comes around after the Board approved it.
When can I renew, and will the board remind me? ▾
The online renewal window opens 59 days before your expiration date; the board emails reminders 59 days and again 14 days before expiration, so keep your email address current in the dashboard.
How much does renewal cost? ▾
$405.00 for online renewal of a two-year license (board renewal instructions, effective August 1, 2023). The underlying fee rule is $200 per year of licensure (N.D. Admin. Code 50-02-07.1-01).
My license just expired. Can I still renew online? ▾
Yes, for up to 3 years after expiration. Log in to your License Dashboard, answer whether you have practiced in North Dakota since the license lapsed, and pay the renewal fees in arrearage ($200 per year past the deadline).
What if my license has been expired 3 years or more? ▾
You must submit a complete new application. Your medical school and postgraduate training verifications from the original file do not need to be resubmitted.
Do I have to prove CME at renewal? ▾
You certify compliance with the 40-hour AMA Category 1 biennial requirement at renewal rather than uploading certificates. The board audits a subset of renewing physicians, who must then submit proof; noncompliance or false certification carries a $500 fee and possible disciplinary action.
