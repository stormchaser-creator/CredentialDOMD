i Deadlines, fees, and what happens if you are late

When it is due
Every year, no later than 30 days after the licensee's birthday, regardless of license issue date (N.C. Gen. Stat. 90-13.2). A licensee's first renewal is due within 30 days of their NEXT birthday, even if that birthday falls shortly after the license was issued.
Fee
$250 (MD and DO, annual renewal; set by N.C. Gen. Stat. 90-13.2 and the board's Renewal Fees schedule, last updated 12.30.2025)
Late fee
$50, added 30 days after the renewal date (birthday) passes, on top of the $250 renewal fee
If you miss the deadline
After the $50 late fee is assessed (30 days past the birthday), the board sends a certified letter and the licensee has an additional 30 days to complete renewal. If renewal is still not completed within that period, the license is automatically placed on inactive status, and it is unlawful to practice with an inactive license. A license inactive for less than one year (with no discipline involved) can be reactivated: application plus $263 fee, fingerprint cards, and a CME log for the inactive period; average processing is 8 weeks. Inactive more than one year, or inactive due to board action, requires full reinstatement, which is essentially a new licensure application. Physicians out of clinical practice for two or more years may also be required to demonstrate competence, determined case by case.

1 Step-by-Step: Renewing Your North Carolina Medical License

Follow these steps to renew your medical license with the North Carolina Medical Board:

  1. Complete your CME for the cycle (60 hours) (see details below)
  2. Log in to the North Carolina Medical Board renewal portal
  3. Complete the renewal application and answer the attestation questions
  4. Pay the renewal fee ($250 (MD and DO, annual renewal; set by N)
  5. Submit and save your confirmation

2 North Carolina CME Requirements

60 hours per 3-year cycle. at least 60 Category 1, 3 hrs controlled substances.

3 Common Pitfalls to Avoid

These are the mistakes North Carolina physicians most commonly make during renewal:

  • The first renewal trap: your first renewal is due within 30 days of your NEXT birthday even if it comes right after your license was issued, and the license application fee does NOT count toward that first renewal fee (board Renewal Fees PDF, 12.30.2025).
  • The lapse clock is fast: $50 late fee at 30 days past your birthday, then a certified letter starts a final 30-day window; miss that and the license goes automatically inactive under N.C. Gen. Stat. 90-13.2, and practicing on an inactive license is unlawful.
  • Reactivation after a lapse is not a quick fix: even under one year inactive it requires a separate application, a $263 fee, fingerprint cards, a CME log for the inactive period, and averages 8 weeks of processing. Over one year inactive means full reinstatement, essentially a new license application.
  • The renewal portal does not support mobile devices; the board says to use a desktop or laptop, and CME hours must be entered as whole numbers with no decimals or extra text or the form errors.
  • Renewal notices go out by email only about two months before your birthday, so a stale email address on file means no reminder. Also, renewing more than 60 days early is possible only by contacting the board's Registration Coordinator.

4 Frequently Asked Questions

When is my North Carolina medical license renewal due? ▾
Every year, no later than 30 days after your birthday, regardless of when your license was issued. This applies to both MDs and DOs, and your first renewal is due within 30 days of your next birthday even if it falls soon after issuance.
How much does renewal cost? ▾
$250 per year for MDs and DOs, paid by credit card through the Licensee Gateway at portal.ncmedboard.org. A $50 late fee is added if you renew more than 30 days after your birthday.
What happens if I miss the deadline entirely? ▾
Thirty days after your birthday the $50 late fee attaches and the board sends a certified letter giving you a final 30 days. If you still have not renewed, your license automatically goes inactive and it is unlawful to practice on it.
How do I get my license back after it goes inactive? ▾
If it has been inactive less than one year and no discipline was involved, apply for reactivation: application, $263 fee, fingerprint cards, and a log of CME earned while inactive; average processing is 8 weeks. Inactive more than one year, or inactive due to board action, requires full reinstatement, essentially a new licensure application. Out of clinical practice 2+ years may also trigger a case-by-case competence assessment.
Is the renewal processed right away? ▾
Yes. Online renewals are processed immediately after the payment shows 'transaction approved,' and the renewal itself typically takes 10-15 minutes. Use a desktop or laptop; the portal does not currently support mobile devices.
What do I have to report at renewal? ▾
You attest to hospital or regulatory board actions, criminal charges or convictions, malpractice suits and settlements, and your AMA PRA Category 1 CME hours earned since your last renewal (NC requires 60 hours per 3-year cycle, including 3 hours on controlled substance prescribing). Enter CME hours as whole numbers only.
