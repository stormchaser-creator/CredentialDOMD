i Deadlines, fees, and what happens if you are late

When it is due
All physician licenses expire March 31 of odd-numbered years (next: March 31, 2027). A renewal notice is emailed to the address on record a few months before expiration.
Late fee
50% of the renewal fee, charged in addition to the renewal fee, for any renewal application submitted after the expiration date (DPR fee page: "The late fee is 50% of the renewal fee.")
If you miss the deadline
There is no grace period; it is illegal to practice in Delaware while the license is expired. A late renewal application may be filed in DELPROS up to one year after the March 31 expiration, paying the renewal fee plus the 50% late fee and certifying that you have not practiced while expired. If not renewed within one year, the license terminates and the physician must reapply in DELPROS as a new applicant (NPDB self-query, Delaware and FBI criminal background checks, service letters from facilities within the past 3 years, license verifications from all jurisdictions, and proof of 40 hours Category 1 CME within the prior two years; the reapplication must be completed within 6 months of starting it).

1 Step-by-Step: Renewing Your Delaware Medical License

Follow these steps to renew your medical license with the Delaware Board of Medical Licensure and Discipline (Division of Professional Regulation):

  1. Complete your CME for the cycle (40 hours) (see details below)
  2. Log in to the Delaware Board of Medical Licensure and Discipline (Division of Professional Regulation) renewal portal
  3. Complete the renewal application and answer the attestation questions
  4. Pay the renewal fee
  5. Submit and save your confirmation

2 Delaware CME Requirements

40 hours per 2-year cycle.

  • At least 40 hours in Category 1
  • 2 hours of controlled substances

3 Common Pitfalls to Avoid

These are the mistakes Delaware physicians most commonly make during renewal:

  • Your Delaware controlled substance registration (CSR) does NOT renew with your medical license. It is a separate DELPROS renewal application on a different clock: CSR expires June 30 of odd-numbered years, the medical license expires March 31 of odd-numbered years, so in a renewal year you have two deadlines three months apart.
  • The late period is not a grace period. DPR states it is illegal to practice in Delaware while your license is expired, and the late renewal application requires you to certify you have not practiced while expired.
  • Miss the one-year late window and the license terminates entirely: you reapply as a new applicant with NPDB self-query, Delaware and FBI fingerprint background checks, service letters, and license verifications, and DELPROS gives you only 6 months to finish the application once you start it.
  • The board does not publish the physician renewal fee amount anywhere on its site; the fee page says you are notified of the amount at the time of renewal inside DELPROS. Third-party sites quote conflicting figures, so budget from what DELPROS actually shows.
  • Renewal notices go only to the email address on record in DELPROS. If your email is stale you may never see the notice, and the expiration date applies regardless.
  • Renewal is attestation-based, but a percentage of licensees are randomly selected for CME audit after renewal, so keep certificates for the full 40-hour requirement on hand.

4 Frequently Asked Questions

When does my Delaware medical license expire? ▾
All Delaware physician (MD and DO) licenses expire on March 31 of odd-numbered years; the current cycle ends March 31, 2027. A renewal notice is emailed to the address on record a few months before expiration.
How do I renew? ▾
Online only, through the DELPROS portal at delpros.delaware.gov. During renewal you attest to completing the required CME and the mandatory training; documents are not uploaded unless you are later selected for audit.
What happens if I miss the March 31 deadline? ▾
You may file a late renewal in DELPROS for up to one year after expiration, paying the renewal fee plus a late fee equal to 50% of the renewal fee, and certifying you have not practiced while expired. Practicing on an expired license is illegal, so you must stop practicing in Delaware until the renewal is granted.
Does renewing my medical license also renew my controlled substance registration? ▾
No. The Delaware CSR is a separate DELPROS renewal application and expires on June 30 of odd-numbered years, a different date from the medical license. DPR's renewal page states that CSR holders must file a separate renewal application.
What CME do I attest to at renewal? ▾
40 hours of Category 1 (AMA or AOA) CME per two-year cycle. CSR holders also need 2 hours in controlled substance prescribing or chronic pain topics each cycle, plus a one-time 1-hour Delaware controlled substance law course within the first year of CSR registration. Starting with the renewal cycle ending March 31, 2027, 2 of the 40 hours must cover Alzheimer's/dementia unless you qualify for the statutory attestation exemption.
Will the board check my CME? ▾
Renewal itself is by attestation, but after renewal a percentage of licensees are randomly selected for a CME audit, so retain your certificates.
