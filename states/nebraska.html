i Deadlines, fees, and what happens if you are late

When it is due
All physician and osteopathic physician licenses expire October 1 of every even-numbered year, regardless of when the license was issued. Renewal application and fee must be postmarked (or submitted online) on or before October 1 of the even year. Next expiration: October 1, 2026.
Fee
$171 total for active status: $121 renewal fee plus a $50 Patient Safety Cash Fund fee (the $50 cannot be waived). Active-Military status: $50 (Patient Safety Cash Fund fee only). Inactive status: $0.
Late fee
Nebraska has no late-renewal fee as such; a missed deadline means the license is expired and must be reinstated. Reinstatement adds a $35 reinstatement fee on top of the $121 renewal fee ($156 total per the fee chart on the 01/2026 reinstatement application, prorated to $65.25 if reinstating April through August of an even-numbered year). Separately, practicing on an expired license incurs an administrative penalty of $10 per day up to $1,000.
If you miss the deadline
No grace period. Per the DHHS renewal page: if you missed your renewal deadline, your license has expired and you cannot practice until it is reissued or reinstated; practicing after expiration incurs a $10 per day administrative penalty up to $1,000. Reinstatement requires the combined renewal + reinstatement fee, 50 Category 1 CE hours already completed within the 24 months before applying, and proof of ONE of the following within the 3 years preceding application: 1 year of active practice (any US state, DC, territory, or Canada), 1 year of approved GME, Board-approved CE, a Board-approved refresher course, or the special purposes examination. The Board of Medicine and Surgery does not routinely accept CE or the special purposes exam alone as evidence of continued competency absent recent practice; under Neb. Rev. Stat. 38-2026.01, a physician who has not actively practiced for the 2 years preceding application may be offered only a reentry license, and declining the reentry license results in denial of the application. A reinstated license expires on the same October 1 of the next even-numbered year.
Processing time
Not same-day. The renewal notice states processing may take up to a week if no additional documentation is required; DHHS explicitly states it cannot provide same-day processing. Renewal status can be checked at dhhs.ne.gov/lookup.

1 Step-by-Step: Renewing Your Nebraska Medical License

Follow these steps to renew your medical license with the Nebraska Department of Health and Human Services, Division of Public Health, Licensure Unit (Board of Medicine and Surgery):

  1. Complete your CME for the cycle (50 hours) (see details below)
  2. Log in to the Nebraska Department of Health and Human Services, Division of Public Health, Licensure Unit (Board of Medicine and Surgery) renewal portal
  3. Complete the renewal application and answer the attestation questions
  4. Pay the renewal fee ($171 total for active status: $121 renewal fee plus a $50 Patient Safety Cash Fund fee (the $50 cannot be waived))
  5. Submit and save your confirmation

2 Nebraska CME Requirements

50 hours per 2-year cycle.

  • At least 50 hours in Category 1
  • 3 hours of opioid prescribing

3 Common Pitfalls to Avoid

These are the mistakes Nebraska physicians most commonly make during renewal:

  • The fixed October 1 even-year expiration applies regardless of issue date, so every Nebraska MD and DO renews at the same time; a physician licensed in mid-2026 faces renewal almost immediately on October 1, 2026.
  • Physicians holding a license issued through the Interstate Medical Licensure Compact must renew via the compact website (imlcc.com), not the Nebraska mylicense.com portal; the DHHS renewal page bolds this restriction.
  • There is no grace period and no simple late fee: one day past October 1 the license is expired, reinstatement paperwork plus a $35 reinstatement fee is required, and each day of practice on the expired license draws a $10/day administrative penalty up to $1,000.
  • A physician who has not actively practiced for roughly 2 to 3 years before reinstating may be offered only a reentry license by the Board of Medicine and Surgery, and the application is denied if the reentry license is not accepted; CE alone does not routinely satisfy the continued-competency requirement.
  • Paper renewals accept only check or money order (no card), while online renewal at nebraska.mylicense.com requires a MasterCard or Visa logo card; DHHS no longer mails a paper license card, so the renewed wallet card must be printed from dhhs.ne.gov/lookup.
  • The $50 Patient Safety Cash Fund fee cannot be waived under any status, including Active-Military (which otherwise pays no renewal fee).

4 Frequently Asked Questions

When does my Nebraska medical license expire? ▾
October 1 of every even-numbered year, regardless of when it was issued. The next expiration is October 1, 2026. Your renewal application and fee must be submitted online or postmarked on or before that date.
How much does renewal cost? ▾
$171 total for an active license: a $121 renewal fee plus a $50 Patient Safety Cash Fund fee that cannot be waived. Active-Military status pays only the $50 fund fee, and inactive status is $0 (but you cannot practice in Nebraska on an inactive license).
How do I renew online? ▾
Through the state portal at nebraska.mylicense.com. You will need your license number, Social Security number, and a credit or debit card with a MasterCard or Visa logo. Exception: if your license was issued through the Interstate Medical Licensure Compact, you must renew through imlcc.com instead.
What happens if I miss the October 1 deadline? ▾
Your license is expired and you cannot practice until it is reinstated. Reinstatement costs the $121 renewal fee plus a $35 reinstatement fee (prorated to $65.25 if reinstating April through August of an even year), requires 50 Category 1 CE hours completed in the prior 24 months, and requires proof of continued competency such as one year of active practice within the last 3 years. Practicing on the expired license draws an administrative penalty of $10 per day up to $1,000.
Will I receive a new license card after renewing? ▾
No. DHHS no longer mails paper renewed license cards. After your renewal processes (up to about a week if no additional documentation is needed), print your wallet card from the license lookup at dhhs.ne.gov/lookup.
Do DOs renew through a separate osteopathic board? ▾
No. Nebraska licenses osteopathic physicians under the same Medicine and Surgery Practice Act, and MDs and DOs use the same renewal notice, the same reinstatement application, the same fees, and the same October 1 even-year cycle through the DHHS Licensure Unit.
