i Deadlines, fees, and what happens if you are late

When it is due
License expires annually on the first day of the month in which the licensee was born (LAC 46:XLV.415: every license "shall annually expire... on the first day of the month in which the licensee was born"). Renewal must be filed on or before that date; the online window opens only 56 days before expiration.
Fee
$332 per year (active MD/DO): $300 renewal fee plus $25 physician health program assessment plus $7 CME assessment under La. R.S. 37:1281. Derived from statute; the board site itself publishes only the $382 initial licensure fee (which is the same $350 + $25 + $7 statutory structure). Reduced by one half for qualifying physicians age 70+ or disabled who have surrendered CDS registration (LAC 46:XLV.418).
Late fee
Not published as a dollar amount by the board. The board states a late fee is assessed for online renewal of a license expired less than 1 year; R.S. 37:1281(A)(3)(c) caps the delinquency fee at an amount equal to the renewal fee, i.e. up to $300 on top of the renewal fee.
If you miss the deadline
No grace period; the license expires on the first day of the birth month. Timely filing of a complete renewal keeps the license in full force while the Board processes it (LAC 46:XLV.415). Expired less than 1 year: renew online via LaMED with a late fee. Expired more than 1 year: full reinstatement application required; reinstatement is available only within 4 years of expiration, with fees of $632 (expired 0-2 yrs), $932 (2-3 yrs), $1,232 (3-4 yrs), plus 20 hours of Category 1 CME for each year since the license was last renewed. Beyond 4 years, re-licensure (new application).

1 Step-by-Step: Renewing Your Louisiana Medical License

Follow these steps to renew your medical license with the Louisiana State Board of Medical Examiners (LSBME):

  1. Complete your CME for the cycle (20 hours) (see details below)
  2. Log in to the Louisiana State Board of Medical Examiners (LSBME) renewal portal
  3. Complete the renewal application and answer the attestation questions
  4. Pay the renewal fee ($332 per year (active MD/DO): $300 renewal fee plus $25 physician health program assessment plus $7 CME assessment under La)
  5. Submit and save your confirmation

2 Louisiana CME Requirements

20 hours per year. at least 20 Category 1, 3 hrs controlled substances, 1 hrs nutrition / metabolic health, 1 hrs sickle cell (emergency medicine).

3 Common Pitfalls to Avoid

These are the mistakes Louisiana physicians most commonly make during renewal:

  • The renewal window opens only 56 days before your expiration date; LaMED will not let you renew earlier, so a very early attempt fails and a late calendar reminder leaves a narrow window.
  • Expiration is the FIRST day of your birth month, not the last; treating the whole birth month as valid means practicing a month on an expired license.
  • All CME must be tracked in CE Broker; the board requires every LSBME licensee to use CE Broker (https://cebroker.com/la/account/basic/), so credits sitting in a personal folder do not count at renewal.
  • No refunds: the board states it does not issue refunds unless you erroneously make multiple payments, and payment is card-only through LaMED.
  • Let it lapse more than 1 year and you are into reinstatement territory: $632 to $1,232 depending on lapse length, 20 hrs Category 1 CME per lapsed year, and reinstatement is only possible within 4 years of expiration; after that you reapply for licensure from scratch.
  • Physicians in training (PGY, GETP, FEL permit holders) do not renew through the online LaMED process; their renewal path is separate.

4 Frequently Asked Questions

When is my Louisiana medical license renewal due? ▾
Every year, on or before the first day of the month in which you were born. LAC 46:XLV.415 provides that every license expires annually on the first day of the licensee's birth month.
How early can I renew? ▾
56 days before your expiration date. The board states on its Renewals page: "You may only renew 56 days before your expiration date." Reminder notices may arrive earlier, but the portal will not accept a renewal before that.
How much does renewal cost? ▾
$332 per year for an active MD/DO: a $300 renewal fee plus statutory $25 and $7 annual assessments under R.S. 37:1281, paid by credit/debit card in LaMED. Physicians age 70+ (or disabled) who have surrendered their controlled substance registrations qualify for a half-price renewal under LAC 46:XLV.418.
What happens if I miss my renewal deadline? ▾
If the license has been expired less than 1 year, you can still renew online through LaMED but will be assessed a late fee (statutorily capped at an amount equal to the renewal fee). If expired more than 1 year, you must file a reinstatement application: $632 if expired up to 2 years, $932 for 2-3 years, $1,232 for 3-4 years, plus 20 hours of Category 1 CME for each year since last renewal. Reinstatement is only available within 4 years of expiration; after that you must reapply for licensure.
Do DOs renew with a separate osteopathic board? ▾
No. Louisiana has no separate osteopathic board; the LSBME licenses and renews both MDs and DOs under the same rules and fees (the board's own fee line reads "Licensure- $382.00 (MD/DO)").
Do I have to use CE Broker? ▾
Yes. The board's Renewals page states that all LSBME licensees are required to use CE Broker to track their continuing education credits; renewal is tied to CME being satisfied there.
