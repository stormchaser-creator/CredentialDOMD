i Deadlines, fees, and what happens if you are late

When it is due
Fixed date: all physician licenses expire January 31 of every year. Renewal information is mailed and/or emailed on or before December 1. A renewal is timely if the fee is received before expiration; forms postmarked February 1 or after are delinquent, except that when January 31 falls on a weekend or legal holiday, a postmark on the next business day is not delinquent (20 CSR 2150-2.001(17)).
Fee
$147 (Permanent Physician Renewal Fee, MD and DO alike, 1-year registration) per 20 CSR 2150-2.080(1)(A)4. All fees are nonrefundable.
Late fee
$25 Late Renewal Fee (Delinquent Fee) per 20 CSR 2150-2.080(1)(B)4; the board may waive it for extenuating circumstances such as illness (section 334.090, RSMo), but failure to receive a renewal notice is expressly not an extenuating circumstance (20 CSR 2150-2.001(8))
If you miss the deadline
No practice grace period: once expired you cannot practice in Missouri until renewal is granted. Late renewal remains possible through MOPRO with the $25 delinquent fee. If registration lapses more than 6 months, the renewal application is denied unless accompanied by all required fees plus a completed renewal application made under oath with full disclosure of disciplinary actions and current physical and mental fitness (20 CSR 2150-2.050); the fee schedule lists a $75 Reinstatement Fee. If the license is not renewed for two consecutive renewal periods it becomes VOID (section 334.080, RSMo); a void license may not be renewed or reactivated (20 CSR 2150-2.001(18)). A license revoked, suspended, or inactive more than 2 years can require 25 hrs CME per lapsed year plus a competency exam (SPEX, specialty board exam, or CPEP/PACE assessment) for reinstatement (20 CSR 2150-2.150); inactive more than 5 years may trigger reexamination (20 CSR 2150-2.153).

1 Step-by-Step: Renewing Your Missouri Medical License

Follow these steps to renew your medical license with the Missouri State Board of Registration for the Healing Arts:

  1. Complete your CME for the cycle (50 hours) (see details below)
  2. Log in to the Missouri State Board of Registration for the Healing Arts renewal portal
  3. Complete the renewal application and answer the attestation questions
  4. Pay the renewal fee ($147 (Permanent Physician Renewal Fee, MD and DO alike, 1-year registration) per 20 CSR 2150-2)
  5. Submit and save your confirmation

2 Missouri CME Requirements

50 hours per 2-year cycle. at least 50 Category 1, 1 hrs nutrition / metabolic health.

3 Common Pitfalls to Avoid

These are the mistakes Missouri physicians most commonly make during renewal:

  • The January 31 expiration applies to every physician license every year regardless of issue date, and a renewal postmarked February 1 or later is delinquent; new licensees get their initial registration through the next renewal date without a registration fee (334.090, RSMo), so a physician licensed in the fall still renews the following January.
  • Failure to receive the renewal notice does not excuse late renewal, and the rule defining extenuating circumstances explicitly excludes it (20 CSR 2150-2.001(8)); address changes must be reported in writing within 15 days (20 CSR 2150-2.045), so a stale address can quietly turn into a delinquent or void license.
  • Miss two consecutive renewal periods and the license becomes VOID under 334.080, RSMo; a void license cannot be renewed or reinstated and requires a completely new licensure application.
  • A lapse over 6 months converts renewal into a sworn application with full disclosure of all disciplinary actions and current physical and mental fitness (20 CSR 2150-2.050), on top of the $75 reinstatement fee; a lapse over 2 years can add 25 CME hours per lapsed year plus SPEX, a specialty board exam, or a CPEP/PACE assessment (20 CSR 2150-2.150).
  • Renewal moved to the new statewide MOPRO portal in January 2025; registering a MOPRO account requires an email address already on record with the board, so physicians who never gave the board an email must contact the board before they can renew online.
  • State tax compliance is a renewal condition: under section 324.010 RSMo, all licensees renewing through the Division of Professional Registration must have paid all Missouri state taxes and filed all required returns for the preceding 3 years.

4 Frequently Asked Questions

When does my Missouri physician license expire? ▾
January 31 of every year, for all physicians. Renewal information is mailed and/or emailed on or before December 1 to the address on file (pr.mo.gov physician application instructions, 375-0457.pdf).
How much does renewal cost? ▾
$147 for a permanent physician license, plus a $25 late renewal (delinquent) fee if the renewal is postmarked February 1 or later. All fees are nonrefundable (20 CSR 2150-2.080).
Where do I renew? ▾
Online through the Division of Professional Registration's MOPRO system at mopro.mo.gov/license/s/, which handles both on-time and late renewals. You must first register a MOPRO account using an email address the board has on record.
Can I keep practicing if I miss the January 31 deadline? ▾
No. The board's own instructions state that if your license expires you cannot practice in Missouri until your renewal is granted. Late renewal with the $25 delinquent fee is available, but practice must stop until it is processed.
What happens if I do not renew for a long time? ▾
Over 6 months lapsed: renewal requires a sworn application with disciplinary and fitness disclosures plus all fees, including the $75 reinstatement fee (20 CSR 2150-2.050). Two consecutive missed renewal periods: the license becomes void and cannot be renewed; you must start over with a new application (334.080, RSMo). Inactive or lapsed over 2 years: the board may require 25 CME hours per lapsed year and a passing SPEX, specialty board, or CPEP/PACE assessment (20 CSR 2150-2.150).
Does renewal require CME? ▾
Yes, but on a separate fixed 2-year clock: 50 hours of Category 1 type credit per biennium running January 1 of an even year through December 31 of the odd year, including 1 hour on nutrition, attested at renewal. The board will not renew without it, though a $25 CME extension fee exists (20 CSR 2150-2.125 and 2.080). ABMS certification or recertification during the period is accepted.
