i Deadlines, fees, and what happens if you are late

When it is due
MD: "Physician licenses expire on January 31 of even years." DO: "Osteopathic Physician and Surgeon license renewals are due May 31st of even years." Controlled substance licenses renew at the same time as the main license. (Next renewal for both: 2028, since the 2026 dates have passed.)
Fee
$193 (MD or DO, 2-year renewal). Add $78 to renew a Utah controlled substance license (total $271). Verified on the FY 7/1/2025-6/30/2026 DOPL fee schedule ("Physician and Surgeon ... Renewal Fee 193.00"; "Osteopathic Physician and Surgeon 200.00 / 193.00"; "Controlled Substance 100.00 / 78.00") and stated as "$193 renewal fee" on the DO renewal page. Fees are non-refundable and "subject to change each July 1."
Late fee
No separate late-renewal window; a lapsed license is a reinstatement. Within 30 days after expiration: renewal fee PLUS $20 for EACH license being reinstated. After 30 days and within 2 years: renewal fee PLUS $50 for EACH license (from the DOPL Renewal/Reinstatement form v.20231107; matches the fee schedule's "Late Renewal Fee $20.00 / Reinstatement Fee $50.00"). The physician license and the controlled substance license are separate licenses, so the per-license fee applies to each.
If you miss the deadline
No grace period: under Utah Code 58-1-308(2)(b) "A license that is not renewed prior to the expiration date shown on the license automatically expires." Reinstatement is available within 2 years of nonrenewal (renewal fee + reinstatement fee + proof of renewal qualifications); a license "reinstated no later than 120 days after it expires shall be retroactively reinstated to the date it expired" (58-1-308(5)(c)). After 2 years you must qualify for a new license (58-1-308(6)(a)), with a 6-month exception for those practicing under federal employment or another state license after reestablishing Utah domicile. Reinstatement cannot be done online; DOPL requires the manual Renewal/Reinstatement form. Practicing after expiration is unlawful conduct and a criminal offense (Utah Code 58-1-501(1)(a), 58-1-502), and DOPL may discipline for time worked unlicensed even after reinstatement.
Processing time
Online renewal via My License One: payment receipt emailed within 2 business days; a copy of the renewed license is emailed 1-3 business days after approval (stated on the DO renewal page; both professions use the same My License One system). No published timeline for paper reinstatements.

1 Step-by-Step: Renewing Your Utah Medical License

Follow these steps to renew your medical license with the Utah Division of Professional Licensing (DOPL), Physicians Licensing Board:

  1. Complete your CME for the cycle (40 hours) (see details below)
  2. Log in to the Utah Division of Professional Licensing (DOPL), Physicians Licensing Board renewal portal
  3. Complete the renewal application and answer the attestation questions
  4. Pay the renewal fee ($193 (MD or DO, 2-year renewal))
  5. Submit and save your confirmation

2 Utah CME Requirements

40 hours per 2-year cycle.

  • At least 34 hours in Category 1
  • 3.5 hours of controlled substances
  • Required topics: Suicide Prevention

3 Common Pitfalls to Avoid

These are the mistakes Utah physicians most commonly make during renewal:

  • MD and DO deadlines are different: MD licenses expire January 31 of even years, DO licenses May 31 of even years. A dual-boarded or converting physician can miss one of the two.
  • The controlled substance license is a separate license renewed at the same time ($78 on top of the $193). If you lapse, the per-license reinstatement penalty ($20 within 30 days, $50 after) is charged for EACH license, so an MD+CS lapse costs double penalties.
  • There is no grace period. The license automatically expires on the expiration date (Utah Code 58-1-308(2)(b)), and continuing to practice is a criminal offense (58-1-501/58-1-502); DOPL can discipline for unlicensed practice even after you reinstate.
  • Reinstatement and re-application cannot be done through the online portal; DOPL requires the paper Renewal/Reinstatement form mailed with fees. Only on-time renewal is online.
  • Renewal notices go only to the email address of record (DOPL emails the notice 60-90 days before expiration), and Utah Code 58-1-301.7 requires you to report any address or email change within 10 business days. An out-of-date email means no renewal notice.
  • Suicide prevention training is a distinct renewal checkbox: you must complete at least one of DOPL's approved online suicide prevention trainings (0.5 credit each) in addition to the 40-hour CME requirement.

4 Frequently Asked Questions

When does my Utah medical license expire? ▾
MD licenses expire January 31 of even-numbered years; DO licenses are due May 31 of even-numbered years. Your controlled substance license renews at the same time as the main license. The exact date is printed on your license.
How much does renewal cost? ▾
$193 for the 2-year MD or DO renewal, plus $78 if you also hold a Utah controlled substance license ($271 total). Fees are non-refundable and reset each July 1 (FY2026 DOPL fee schedule).
How do I renew online? ▾
Log in to My License One (utahdoc.mylicenseone.com) with your UtahID, find your license under 'Existing License/Registration Holders' (use the 'Don't see your license' link if needed), and start the renewal from the Actions menu. DOPL notes the process works best on a computer, not a mobile device.
What happens if I miss the deadline? ▾
The license automatically expires with no grace period. Within 30 days you reinstate by paying the renewal fee plus $20 per license; after 30 days and within 2 years, renewal fee plus $50 per license, using the paper Renewal/Reinstatement form (not the online portal). If reinstated within 120 days, the license is retroactive to the expiration date. After 2 years you must qualify as a new applicant.
What CME do I need to renew? ▾
40 hours per 2-year cycle, at least 34 hours Category 1 (AOA or ACCME for DOs), per R156-67-304 (MD) / R156-68-304 (DO); 3.5 hours of controlled substance prescribing CE if you hold a CS license (Utah Code 58-37-6.5, R156-37-402); and at least one of DOPL's approved online suicide prevention trainings (0.5 credit each, count toward CE). DOPL audits on a random-sample basis.
Can I keep practicing while my reinstatement is processing? ▾
No. Practicing after expiration is unlawful conduct and a criminal offense under Utah Code 58-1-501(1)(a) and 58-1-502, and DOPL may take disciplinary action for any period worked without a license even after it is renewed or reinstated. The only protection is the retroactive effect of a reinstatement completed within 120 days.
