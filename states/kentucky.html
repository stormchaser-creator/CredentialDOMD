Fixed date for all licensees: renewal is due by March 1 of each year. Reminder notices are mailed to all active licensees in late December. Renewals paid after March 1 but before April 1 require a penalty fee; failure to renew by April 1 makes the license automatically inactive.

Late fee
$50 penalty if paid March 1 to April 1; $100 penalty after April 1 (at which point the license has also automatically become inactive). Per KBML Board Fees PDF and 201 KAR 9:041.

Late window March 1 to April 1 with a $50 penalty on top of the $150 fee. Reactivation cannot be done online: the physician must contact the Board at (502) 764-2607 or teresa.kleinhenz@ky.gov for a re-registration form; the fee for re-registration of an inactive license is $250. The online application is for new applicants only.

1 Step-by-Step: Renewing Your Kentucky Medical License

Follow these steps to renew your medical license with the Kentucky Board of Medical Licensure (KBML):

  1. Complete your CME for the cycle (60 hours) (see details below)
  2. Log in to the Kentucky Board of Medical Licensure (KBML) renewal portal
  3. Complete the renewal application and answer the attestation questions
  4. Pay the renewal fee ($150 (annual registration or renewal of regular MD/DO license, online))
  5. Submit and save your confirmation

2 Kentucky CME Requirements

60 hours per 3-year cycle. at least 30 Category 1, 4.5 hrs controlled substances, 3 hrs domestic violence, 1 hrs pediatric abusive head trauma.

3 Common Pitfalls to Avoid

These are the mistakes Kentucky physicians most commonly make during renewal:

  • The wallet card is print-it-now-or-lose-it: the Board no longer mails wallet cards, the only one provided must be printed at the end of the online renewal, and once the receipt screen is closed it cannot be reproduced (Board renewal page).
  • IMLC compact license holders must NOT renew through the KBML portal: licenses issued through the Interstate Medical Licensure Compact are renewed on the IMLCC website (imlcc.org/renew), per both the Board renewal page and the Winter 2026 newsletter.
  • Do not create a new account on the eBusiness portal: the login page warns renewing licensees already have one (username = KY license number, password = last 4 of SSN); creating a new profile derails the renewal.
  • The Board's own renewal page (kbml.ky.gov/physician/Pages/Physician-Renewal.aspx) returned HTTP 401 when loaded on 2026-08-19; it appears to be taken down outside the roughly January to April renewal season, so bookmark the eBusiness portal login directly.
  • Affirmative answers (malpractice, DUI, board action) require attached legal documentation and Board review; the Board explicitly warns not to wait until the deadline because review can delay the renewal.
  • Missing April 1 is expensive and offline: the license goes automatically inactive, reactivation requires contacting the Board for a paper re-registration form, and the re-registration fee is $250 versus the normal $150.

4 Frequently Asked Questions

When is my Kentucky medical or osteopathic license renewal due? ▾
By March 1 every year. Reminder notices are mailed to all physicians holding an active license in late December, and the online renewal is completed through the Board's eBusiness portal. Renewals paid after March 1 but before April 1 require an extra $50 penalty; after April 1 the license automatically becomes inactive.
How much does renewal cost? ▾
$150 for annual registration or renewal of a regular license. Renewing by paper application adds $10. The late penalty is $50 between March 1 and April 1, and $100 after April 1. Limited license renewal is $65. All amounts are from the Board Fees PDF, which mirrors 201 KAR 9:041.
How do I log in to renew online? ▾
Go to the KBML eBusiness portal (services.kbml.ky.gov/ebusiness). Your username is your Kentucky license number and your password is the last four digits of your SSN. Do not create a new account; renewing licensees already have one. Payment is by credit card or ACH debit, and the payment screen opens as a pop-up, so allow pop-ups.
What happens if I miss the April 1 deadline? ▾
The license automatically becomes inactive and continued practice in Kentucky is considered the unauthorized practice of medicine or osteopathy. You cannot reactivate online: contact the Board at (502) 764-2607 or teresa.kleinhenz@ky.gov for a re-registration form. The re-registration fee for an inactive license is $250.
I hold an IMLC compact license. Do I renew with KBML? ▾
No. The Board states that physicians holding a license issued through the Interstate Medical Licensure Compact must renew on the IMLCC website (imlcc.org/renew), not through the KBML portal.
How do I prove I am renewed while waiting for the record to update? ▾
Print the temporary wallet card and receipt at the completion of online payment by choosing the Finish Transaction With KBML option; the Board no longer mails wallet cards and the card cannot be reproduced once the receipt screen closes. The renewed license appears on the Board's online verification site in approximately 48 hours.
