Minnesota nurse practitioner license renewal

What the Minnesota Board of Nursing requires to renew, checked against the board's own rules in October 2026. Every figure below links to its source.

A nurse practitioner renews two licenses: the RN license and the APRN license, which some states call a certificate or a registration. The rules for each are below.

Minnesota APRN license (nurse practitioner)

How often this license renews is not yet verified. Check with the Minnesota Board of Nursing.

Continuing education

The continuing education requirement verified for this license is keeping national certification current. Minn. Stat. 148.231 subd. 1(c)

Certification and practice

  • Current national certification is required at renewal. Minn. Stat. 148.231 subd. 1(c)

Minnesota RN license

The license renews every 2 years. Minn. R. 6310.2600 subp. 15

Continuing education

24 contact hours every 2 years. Minn. R. 6310.2800 subp. 3 Minn. R. 6310.2600 subp. 15

  • Current certification from a national professional nursing organization documents the CE learning objectives. Minn. R. 6310.2800 subp. 4a
  • The hours are one contact hour for each month of registration: 24 in a 24-month period, and in the first registration period (six to 29 months) one per month of that period. Minn. R. 6310.2800 subp. 3

Certification and practice

  • Minnesota is not a Nurse Licensure Compact state. SF 2608 status, 94th Legislature

Practice agreement and prescribing

What the board's rules say a nurse practitioner keeps in place while licensed:

  • Collaborative agreement. An NP first practices at least 2,080 hours under a collaborative agreement; since August 1, 2026 that must last at least three years with an APRN collaborator, who may be a physician or an APRN with at least three years of APRN practice. An NP whose first 2,080 hours are in services other than primary care or mental health completes them in a setting where APRNs and physicians work together. Minn. Stat. 148.211 subd. 1c 2026 Minn. Laws c. 115 art. 11 s. 1 Minn. Stat. 645.02 2026 Minn. Laws c. 115 art. 11 s. 2

National certification and federal rules

  • National certification. Each certifying body sets its own renewal rules: AANPCB, ANCC, PNCB, NCC.
  • DEA registration. Every DEA registrant attests once to 8 hours of training on treating and managing patients with opioid or other substance use disorders. PA or advanced practice nursing graduates within 5 years whose curriculum had 8 or more hours of substance use disorder training satisfy it. DEA MATE Act Q&A

Not yet verified

We could not confirm these in the board's own rules, so this guide gives no figure for them. Ask the board.

  • Fetal alcohol syndrome education for APRNs with primary responsibility for diagnosing and treating it (Minn. Stat. 214.12 subd. 3; the board rule was not loaded). Minnesota Board of Nursing

What this guide does not cover

The renewal fee and the date your own license expires are not in this guide. Get both from the Minnesota Board of Nursing.
