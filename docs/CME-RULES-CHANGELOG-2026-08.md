# CME rules changelog, 2026-08 recheck

Primary-source recheck of every jurisdiction in `src/constants/stateRequirements.js`, run 2026-08-17. Method: load the regulator's own page or the codified rule, corroborate with a second official source where possible, then apply only the changes that were confirmed. Every jurisdiction that was confirmed or changed now carries `verified: "2026-08"` and a `sourceUrl` (the best official page loaded). Rules that are enacted or pending but not yet in force live in a per-entry `upcoming` array and show as "Coming:" under the provenance line in the app.

Counts: 55 jurisdictions, 68 rule sets (13 states run separate MD and DO boards). 67 of 68 rule sets stamped verified 2026-08. 1 unverifiable (VI). Meta: `lastReviewed` 2026-08, `reviewMethod` "primary-source recheck, every jurisdiction, 2026-08-17".

Legend: confirmed = no change to the numbers or mandates. changed = what moved. unverifiable = which URL failed. needs a human look = a proposed change that was not applied because the sources conflict or a claim could not be confirmed.

## App changes in this pass

- `STATE_REQS_META`: `lastReviewed` 2026-08, `reviewMethod` added.
- Entries: `verified`, `sourceUrl`, `upcoming` fields; `getStateReq` and `DEFAULT_STATE_REQ` carry them; `computeCompliance` returns `sourceUrl` and `upcoming` alongside `source` and `verified`.
- `RuleProvenance`: citation renders as a link when `sourceUrl` is an http(s) URL; a "Coming:" line lists `upcoming` items.
- `CMESection`: passes `sourceUrl` and `upcoming` to `RuleProvenance`; the "Required by your states" tag list now includes zero-hour required topics, so state-specific checklist items outside the general topic list (MI opioid awareness, TX Life of the Mother Act) can be tagged and met.
- New topic names introduced by the recheck that are not in `CME_TOPICS` (they appear as taggable "Required by your states" chips but the document scanner does not know them): Sexual Assault, Behavioral Health, Florida Laws and Rules, Dependent Adult Abuse, Alzheimer's Disease and Other Dementias, Pediatric Abusive Head Trauma, Nutrition / Metabolic Health, Sickle Cell (Emergency Medicine), Electronic Health Records, Opioid Awareness, HIV Stigma, Organ and Tissue Donation, Dengue, Hypertension, Diabetes, Life of the Mother Act.
