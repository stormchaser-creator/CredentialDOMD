# CredentialDOMD — Physician FAQ

> Generated by Scout agent (via AutoAIBiz SDK bridge, 2026-04-05). Cost: $0.0473.

---

**Q: What types of credentials can I track?**
CredentialDOMD covers the full spectrum of physician credentials in one place: state medical licenses, CME credits, DEA registrations, board certifications, hospital privileges, malpractice insurance, and health/immunization records. No more spreadsheets or missed renewal dates — everything is visible on a single dashboard with expiration alerts.

---

**Q: Do you support both MD and DO credentials?**
Yes. CredentialDOMD tracks AMA PRA Category 1 and Category 2 credits for MDs as well as AOA Category 1-A, 1-B, and 2 credits for DOs. Whichever path you trained on, your CME is tracked correctly.

---

**Q: I hold licenses in multiple states — can you track different CME requirements for each?**
Absolutely. Each state license has its own CME hour requirements, cycle lengths, and subject mandates (opioid training, ethics, etc.), and CredentialDOMD tracks them independently. You'll always know exactly what's required and what you've completed for every state where you're licensed.

---

**Q: Is my data secure?**
Your credential data is stored in Supabase with encryption at rest and TLS encryption in transit, and row-level security isolates your records to your account. Note two limits we state plainly: we do not yet keep an access audit trail, and support tickets and feedback you send are read by the founder.

---

**Q: Is CredentialDOMD HIPAA compliant?**
**CredentialDOMD is not HIPAA compliant and does not sign business associate agreements.** It is built to hold your own credential, billing, and case records — not patient health information. Keep patient identifiers out of it. The AI features send what you give them to Google's Gemini API under your own key; Google's free tier permits human review of submitted content.

---

**Q: What happens to my data if I cancel?**
You can export your full credential history at any time — your data is yours, full stop. There's no lock-in, no hostage data, and no fees to retrieve what you've entered.

---

**Q: How is this different from CAQH ProView?**
CAQH ProView is built for hospitals and payers to pull your credentials — you maintain it for their benefit. CredentialDOMD is built for *you*: a physician-owned record that you control, with proactive alerts and tools designed around how you actually manage your career.

---

**Q: What does "Founding Physician" mean?**
Founding Physician pricing is available to the first 100 physicians only. You lock in **$12/month (or $120/year)** for 24 months; at month 25 your plan converts to the standard Solo rate ($19/month). You also get early access to new features and a direct line to shape the product roadmap.

---

**Q: Can my practice use CredentialDOMD for multiple providers?**
Yes. Multi-provider plans are billed annually per provider: **Practice** is $39/provider/month for 2–25 seats, **Group** is $29/provider/month for 26–100 seats, and **Enterprise** (100+ seats) is available by contacting sales.

**Q: How much does it cost?**
There's a **free plan** ($0, up to 5 credentials, free forever). Paid individual plans are **Solo at $19/month ($190/year)** and **Locum at $29/month ($290/year)** — both include a 14-day trial, and annual billing gives you two months free.
