# CredentialDoMD — Physician FAQ

Updated September 19, 2026. Billing remains off during the invite-only beta.

## Who is CredentialDoMD for?

Physicians who want their credentials and locums business records in one place. Review the rates extracted from your contract, log your work, build an invoice, and record full or partial payments to see the remaining balance. Your licenses, DEA registrations, CME, certifications and hospital privileges sit alongside that workflow.

## What can I track?

State medical licenses, DEA registrations, board certifications, CME, hospital privileges, liability coverage and professional health clearances. The locums tools include contracts, work logs, invoices, payments, expenses and finance planning. AI-extracted fields and billing details need your review before use.

## Do you support both MDs and DOs?

Yes. Credential tracking includes MD and DO CME categories and state-specific renewal cycles. Confirm your final renewal requirements with the issuing board; the app helps organize your record and identify gaps.

## Can I track more than one state and agency?

Yes. Each license has its own dates and CME cycle, and contracts, work logs and invoices stay associated with the relevant assignment. Payment tracking shows what you recorded as collected and what remains outstanding. State tax planning models cover a smaller set of states than credential tracking; the app labels unsupported tax models.

## How do I track payments?

The current workflow builds invoices from your reviewed contract terms and work entries. You record the agency's payments, including partials, then check the balance against your invoice.

## Will the Practice trial charge me automatically?

Credential customers will receive a free 30-day Practice trial. It ends without a charge. Continuing Practice requires an explicit purchase; your Credential membership continues. Billing is off today, so no trial countdown starts from reviewing these planned offers.

## What happens to existing registered accounts?

Under the planned lifetime-access policy, accounts already registered when the policy was announced will keep Credential and Practice free for life. A waitlist entry alone does not qualify. Account eligibility will be confirmed before billing opens.

## Do I need to buy an AI API key?

No. Approved beta accounts can use shared AI access, subject to availability and usage limits. Gemini is the default for Vera, and Claude is available where selected and enabled. You may optionally supply your own Gemini or Anthropic key in Settings; that key stays on the device and provider charges are your responsibility.

## Where does my information go?

Professional records and uploaded documents sync to Supabase; Clerk manages sign-in. The operator has administrative access for support and operating the service. AI features send the content you submit to Google or Anthropic. The assistant can also receive a summary of your records, conversation context and attachments. Support messages and assistant activity logs are available to the operator and may be handled with AI tooling.

The private note field on a work or case entry uses separate browser storage. Those notes do not automatically sync or go into invoices, AI requests or cloud backups. They are not separately encrypted by the app. Read [Security and data handling](https://credentialdomd.com/security) and the [Privacy Policy](https://credentialdomd.com/privacy).

## Is it HIPAA compliant?

We make no HIPAA compliance claim and do not offer a BAA. Keep patient identifiers out of synced fields, uploads, support tickets, chats and dictation. Screening and AI instructions cannot guarantee their removal. A local private note does not protect information you submit elsewhere. Do not use the service for a workflow requiring a BAA.

## Can I export my records?

Yes. Data & Backup provides record exports, and invoices and other reports have their own export options. Private notes require a separate manual export and restore. Keep all exports private; the private-note file is readable. Export local notes before signing out, clearing browser storage or changing devices.

## Can I work offline?

Cached records and local work logging are available after signing in on that device. Changes sync when the connection returns. AI and document files that are not already available on the device require a connection.

## Can my practice manager use it for our whole group?

The planned Credential and Credential + Practice memberships are for one physician. Group workflows are not a promised feature of the individual launch. Contact support about your needs before choosing a tool for a whole practice.

## Does it replace CAQH or my accountant?

You can organize credentials for your CAQH updates and export business records for your accountant. CredentialDoMD does not submit a renewal or tax return on your behalf. Review credentials with the issuing body and financial estimates with your preparer.
